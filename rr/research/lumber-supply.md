# Lumber & Material Supply — Build Research (Reimagine Ranch)

**Status:** Research — 2026-05-29. Internal. Anchored on the **compost latrine** (RR's first build,
see [`compost-latrine.md`](./compost-latrine.md) §5 materials & §7 locked dimensions). The sourcing
strategy here is written to **generalize to later RR builds** (deck, shade structure, ramp, wash
station), because the species, suppliers, and procurement rhythm repeat.

> RR posture: **build slowly, with as much community labor and local material as possible.** The
> lumber plan is part of the sustainability story, not just a cost line — *locally sourced* is one
> of the fundable adjectives the latrine genuinely earns. Lead with the salvaged-local-wood angle.

---

## 0. The decision in one line

Buy **structural framing + foundation hardware off-the-shelf** (fast, cheap, code-known), and
buy the **cedar/cypress touch-and-show surfaces from a local Austin salvage sawmill** (the story,
the sustainability, the grant photo). Order the **metal roof early** (longest lead). Everything
else is in-stock or same-week.

---

## 1. What the build actually needs (species by use)

From `compost-latrine.md` §5, mapped to what to buy:

| Assembly | Material | Why this, not that |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Precast deck piers **or** helical screw piles | No concrete pour — reversible, low-carbon, community-installable |
| Substructure (sills, beams, joists) | **Pressure-treated SYP** (ground-contact) | Off-soil framing must resist rot/termite; hidden, so PT is fine |
| Decking + floor (touch surface) | **Cedar or cypress** 5/4×6 | Rot/insect resistance with **no toxic treatment** where people stand |
| Stall wall framing | SPF/SYP 2×4 (untreated, above deck) | Hidden studs; cheap, straight |
| Siding (board-and-batten) | **Cedar** 1× (salvage/local ideal) | Breathable, repairable, the visible face — the story |
| Crown vent, fascia, trim | **Cedar** 1×/2× | Matches siding, weathers gracefully |
| Roof structure | SYP 2×6 rafters + 2×4 purlins | Metal-on-purlin (skip plywood deck) — lighter, cheaper, ag-standard |
| Roof skin | **Standing-seam or exposed-fastener metal** | Longevity, fire-wise, **rainwater-harvest ready** (feeds the tank upgrade) |
| Ramp / stair / rails | PT stringers + **cedar** treads, caps, balusters | Structure hidden-PT, hand-touch cedar |
| Fasteners | **Stainless or hot-dip galvanized** | Survive the damp + cedar's acidity (plain steel stains/streaks) |
| Finish | Tung or boiled linseed oil (or leave raw) | Low-VOC, re-applyable; cedar silvers handsomely if left raw |

**Cedar note for Texas:** *Western Red Cedar* (WRC) is the stocked retail species at Austin yards
(decking/siding grades, kiln-dried, straight). *Eastern Red Cedar* ("Texas cedar"/Ashe juniper) is
the **local, salvaged, story** species — gorgeous, aromatic, rot-resistant, but knottier, often
green (needs drying), and milled to order. **Use both:** WRC where you need long clean runs
(decking, rails); local Eastern Red where character + sourcing story matter most (siding, crown).
Cypress is the middle path — Gulf-region native, stable, available through Texas sawmills.

a hardware/plumbing add, costed separately in §4.

## 3. Where to buy it (Central Texas)

Ordered by RR fit — **local & salvaged first**, then yards, then chains for the commodity framing.

### Tier 1 — Local salvage sawmill (the story + the cedar/cypress)
- **Harvest Lumber Co.** — central east Austin urban sawmill. Mills **fallen/felled Central Texas
  trees** (cedar, cypress, oak, mesquite, pecan, pine) that would otherwise be chipped/burned;
  partners with arborists and the City of Austin and **tracks every log's origin** ("a slab with a
  story"). Rough-sawn dimensional by the board foot. → *This is the grant-photo, mission-aligned
  source for siding + crown + character pieces.* Showroom Tue–Sat. ([harvestlumberco.com](https://www.harvestlumberco.com/))
- **Berdoll Sawmill** (Cedar Creek, ~SE of Austin) — kiln-dried native Texas lumber, slabs.
  ([berdollsawmill.com](https://berdollsawmill.com/))
- **Southwest Trading Post** (Austin) — locally-sourced, **cut-to-order and kiln-dried on site**,
  natural wood siding + trim milled at the mill. ([swtpinc.com](https://swtpinc.com/materials))
- **M&G Sawmill** (near Huntsville, ~2.5 hr) — rough-cut native hardwood/softwood incl. aromatic
  cedar; worth a quote for volume but freight matters. ([mgsawmill.com](https://www.mgsawmill.com/))

### Tier 2 — Austin specialty lumber yards (stocked WRC decking/siding + cypress)
- **Eastside Lumber & Decking** (Austin) — Western Red Cedar for siding/trim/milling, in stock.
  ([eastsidelumberaustin.com](https://www.eastsidelumberaustin.com/cedar-lumber-austin/))
- **TimberTown Austin** — cedar to builders/designers; decking grades.
  ([timbertown.com](https://www.timbertown.com/cedar-austin-texas/))

### Tier 3 — Commodity framing, PT, fasteners, fast (chains)
- **McCoy's Building Supply** (Texas chain), **Builders FirstSource** (Austin yard), Home Depot /
  Lowe's — PT SYP framing, SPF studs, hangers, SS/HDG fasteners, gravel, oil. In stock, same-week.
  ([bldr.com Austin](https://www.bldr.com/location/austin-tx-lumber-yard/AUSTTXYD))

### Roof (metal)
- Standing-seam or exposed-fastener **ag-panel** from a Texas metal supplier (McElroy, Mueller,
  or a local roll-former). Order **early** — see lead times below.

### Foundation
- Precast pier blocks (any yard) for the DIY/no-pour path; **helical/screw piles** via a Texas
  installer (e.g. Techno Metal Post / local) if going engineered.

## 5. Lead times & projected timing

The schedule driver is **two long poles**: (1) metal roof, (2) green-cedar drying — everything else
is in-stock or installed within a couple weeks.

| Item | Lead time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commodity framing, PT, fasteners, gravel | **same day** | in stock at chains |
| WRC decking/siding (yards) | **days** | in stock |
| Local salvage cedar/cypress, **green** | **3–8 weeks** | if not already dry — air/kiln drying. Order FIRST or buy already-dried |
| Standing-seam metal, standard color | **4–8 weeks** (2026) | improved from 12–16 wk in 2023; custom colors longer |
| Exposed-fastener ag-panel | **days–2 weeks** | often stocked |
| Screw-pile install (if Path B) | **2–3 weeks** | schedule the installer crew |
| Precast piers (DIY) | **same day** | — |

**Procurement-to-build projected timeline** (weekend community cadence, Path A):

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0 | Confirm design + path; **order metal roof** + **reserve/dry local cedar** (long poles) |
| 1 | Buy framing/PT/fasteners/gravel; set out piers; site prep |
| 1–2 | **Build session 1:** footings + substructure + deck |
| 3 | **Build session 2:** stall walls + framing |
| 4–5 | Metal arrives → **Build session 3:** roof structure + skin + vent stacks |
| 5–6 | Cedar dry/ready → **Build session 4:** siding, crown, fascia, ramp + stair + rails |
| 6–7 | **Build session 5:** skirt + hatches, fixtures, grab bars, signage, oil finish |
| 7–8 | Punch list, first use; (rainwater upgrade can bolt on here or later) |

**~6 community work sessions over ~6–8 calendar weeks**, gated by the metal-roof and cedar-drying
lead times rather than by labor. Path B adds ~1–2 weeks for the screw-pile crew + finish carpentry.

## 6. Sustainability sourcing story (for grants / the site tour)

Every claim here is true of the actual plan:
- **Locally sourced & salvaged** — siding/crown from Austin trees that would have been chipped or
  burned, with **documented log provenance** (Harvest Lumber). A board with a story.
- **No concrete** (Path A) — precast/reversible piers; ground-disturbance story intact.
- **No toxic treatment where touched** — cedar/cypress, oil finish; PT confined to hidden structure.
- **Rainwater-ready roof** — the metal skin feeds the cistern + shower upgrade; closes a water loop.
- **Repairable & breathable** — board-and-batten cedar, swappable boards, no sealed assemblies.
- **Community-installable** — no crane, no pour; hand tools + a pier driver + a weekend crew.

## 7. Open decisions (feed the proposal)

1. **Cedar species split:** how much local Eastern Red (story, may need drying) vs stocked WRC
   (fast, clean)? Recommendation: **local for siding/crown, WRC for decking/rails.**
2. **Green vs kiln-dried local cedar:** green is cheaper + more available but needs 3–8 wk drying
   and will move; buy already-dried if the schedule is tight.
3. **Foundation:** precast piers (Path A, cheap, no-pour, DIY) vs screw piles (Path B, engineered,
   +$2.4–4k installed). Recommendation: **precast for the pilot.**
4. **Roof:** true standing-seam (premium, longest lead) vs exposed-fastener ag-panel (budget, fast).
   Both are rainwater-clean. Recommendation: **ag-panel for the pilot, standing-seam if grant-funded.**
5. **Ramp length:** compact massing run vs **true 1:12** (≈22.5 ft, more decking + PT). Accessibility
   posture says honor 1:12; budget +~$150–250 in lumber.
6. **Quantity confidence:** these are estimates; the cut list (FreeCAD step) firms them before the
   final buy.
