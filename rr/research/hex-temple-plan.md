# The Hex Temple — Sacred-Geometry Composting Toilet, Design Plan

**Status:** Design plan — 2026-05-31. Internal. The fun/vision plan for the hexagonal toroidal
composting toilet (`rr/blender/hex_toilet.py`). Written in Reimagine Ranch voice — sacred geometry,
phi proportion, Earth-based materials, sovereignty — but every claimed function is **actually true
of the build** (RR's Integrity law: honest materials, honest words).

> **The reframe:** it isn't "a toilet." It's a **threshold** — a small temple that turns a daily
> necessity into a moment of remembrance. You step through a glowing portal, the room breathes,
> the torus draws the air up and out, and what you return goes back to the living soil. A beacon
> on the land by night, a sundial-lantern by day.

---

## The idea in one line

A **hexagon** (the Flower-of-Life cell), proportioned by **phi**, capped by a **toroidal vortex
roof** that sweeps up to a **glowing oculus** — the torus throat that *is* the ventilation, the
skylight, and the night beacon — standing on a **raised platform over a gemstone-grid node**, built
from **charred + natural cedar and copper**, returning nutrients to the Earth.

### Fun / experiential layer (the delight)
- **Night beacon** — solar LED oculus ring + louver light-leak → glows across the land for ceremony/wayfinding.
- **Star slot** — by night the oculus frames the sky; by day it's a moving sun-spot inside (a living sundial).
- **Breath you can feel** — the passive torus updraft is real airflow; a small **vent chime** in the throat sings when it draws.
- **Aroma** — aromatic cedar + the charred-wood scent; herbs in the base planter.
- **A line to read** — a single engraved remembrance line on the door under the brand (e.g. *"Return what you borrow to the living Earth."*).
