What we do

Two chairs, one standard: get it right.

Most folks come in for a fade or a beard. Plenty come in for color and a blowout. We do all of it, and we take our time on every head that sits down.

Fades, tapers & beard work

The signature. Skin fades, tapers, line-ups, and a beard trim that frames the whole thing. "Best fades in town, hands down" is a real review, not our words.

Kids' haircuts

Patient with the little ones, even the squirmy ones. Parents come back because the first cut goes well.

Freehand designs & hard parts

Want a part carved in, a star, a clean piece of line work? The clippers do more than fades here.

Color & balayage

Soft highlights and natural balayage, or full bold fashion color: purple, teal, green, blue. Sherry and the team are not afraid of a vivid box.

Women's cuts, layers & curtain bangs

Shapes that grow out well and layers that move. Curtain bangs done so they actually sit right.

Blowouts & styling

Smooth, full, and finished. The kind of blowout that makes a regular out of a first-timer.

The shop
