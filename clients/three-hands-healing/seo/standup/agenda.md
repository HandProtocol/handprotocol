# SEO standup: Three Hands Healing

- **When:** date to be set · about 79 minutes
- **Site:** https://three-hands-healing.netlify.app/
- **Last audit:** 2026-10-01, 92/100, launch gate blocked

## Prepare before the session

- Re-run the audit the day before; fix every finding marked "HAND can fix now".
- Have the verification-code field open in /admin/ (Search & sharing) so the code goes live in the session.
- Draft the Business Profile category, service-area list and a 750-character description for the owner to approve.
- Ask the owner to have this login ready: Namecheap (for 3handshealing.com)
- Ask the owner to have this login ready: systeme.io (to export your email list before the domain moves)

## Agenda

### 0–5 min · Hello and the plan

- Say what the session is for: the steps only the owner can do, done together, so the site can be found.
- Show the current audit score (92/100, launch gate blocked) and what HAND already fixed.

### 5–15 min · Facts for the site and the listings

- Do you want a phone number on the site? If yes, which number, and is it OK for texts?
- Which email should enquiries go to?
- How should people book: a calendar link, a call, a text, or an email?
- What are your session prices (or should prices stay off the site for now)?
- Which hours or days do you take sessions, or is it by appointment only?
- Write each answer down with the exact spelling; record it with: seo.mjs facts <slug> set key=value

### 15–23 min · Google Search Console: property verified, sitemap submitted

- Owner: Sign in to Google with the business account (or make one).
- Owner: Open search.google.com/search-console and press Add property.
- Owner: Choose URL prefix, paste the site address HAND gives you, press Continue.
- Owner: Choose HTML tag, press Copy, and send HAND the code (or paste it into the Setup tab).
- Owner: After HAND publishes it, press Verify.
- Owner: Open Sitemaps, type sitemap.xml, press Submit.
- Owner: Done when it says "Ownership verified" and the sitemap shows "Success".
- HAND: Publish the verification code (engine sites: /admin/ → Search & sharing → Google verification).
- HAND: Re-run the audit after the sitemap is submitted.
- HAND: Once the domain moves, add the new domain as a property too and use **Change of address** if the old one was ever indexed.

### 23–43 min · Google Business Profile: created or claimed, verified

- Owner: Open business.google.com and press Manage now.
- Owner: Search for the business name. If it exists, press Claim; if not, press Add your business.
- Owner: Pick the closest category (HAND will suggest one).
- Owner: For a business that visits clients or works by appointment only, choose to hide the address and list the service area.
- Owner: Add the phone and the website address.
- Owner: Verify. Google usually asks for a short video: the outside of the place, the sign or tools of the trade, and something only the owner can open. Record it in one take.
- Owner: Done when the profile says "Verified".
- HAND: Prepare the category, service area, description and photos before the session.
- HAND: Keep the name, phone and city exactly the same as on the website.
- HAND: After verification, copy the profile link and the review link for the site.

### 43–51 min · Review link: copied from the Business Profile, on the site

- Owner: In the Business Profile, press Ask for reviews, then Copy link.
- Owner: Send the link to HAND.
- Owner: Done when the link opens the review box.
- HAND: Put the review link on the site (a "Leave a review" link near the contact block).
- HAND: Add the profile to the structured data sameAs list.
- HAND: Never mark up the business's own reviews as stars on its site: Google ignores self-serving reviews.

### 51–59 min · Bing Webmaster Tools: imported from Search Console

- Owner: Open bing.com/webmasters and sign in with the same Google account.
- Owner: Press Import next to "Import your sites from GSC".
- Owner: Done when the site shows in the list.
- HAND: If import is not possible, publish the msvalidate.01 code instead.

### 59–69 min · The domain move

- Owner: Export anything the old site holds before DNS changes (email list, form entries, blog posts).
- Owner: Share access to the domain registrar (or log in together at the standup).
- Owner: Add the new domain in Search Console and submit its sitemap (HAND publishes the new verification code).
- Owner: Update the website link on the Business Profile and every social profile.
- Login needed: Namecheap (for 3handshealing.com)
- Login needed: systeme.io (to export your email list before the domain moves)

### 69–74 min · Audit items only the owner can settle

- there is no way to contact or book: no phone link, email link, form or booking link anywhere on the site (the "Book a session" button only scrolls the page) (Get one real contact route from the owner (a booking link, a phone number, or an email) and wire the main button to it.)
- the page tells visitors it is not finished: "n · Sessions in Texas Booking and contact details will be added at launch getting healthier and happier Three Hands Heal" (Get the missing detail from the owner (booking link, phone, prices) and replace the line.)
- no phone number on the site (Ask the owner whether they take calls or texts. If yes, add the number as a tel: link; if no, leave it out on purpose.)
- HealthAndBeautyBusiness at @graph[1]: "telephone" is missing (Confirm the fact with the owner, then add it. Never fill it with a guess.)

### 74–79 min · Wrap up

- Read back every fact collected and every account created.
- Agree who does what next, and by when.
- After the session: record facts, tick Google items (seo.mjs google <slug> --done=<item>), re-run the audit.
