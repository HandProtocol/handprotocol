Getting Three Hands Healing found on Google

date to be set · about an hour, together with HAND

Your website is built and HAND has done the technical setup. A few steps can only be done by you, because Google needs the business owner to confirm who they are. We will do them together, one at a time. Nothing to prepare beyond the list below.

What we will do

  • Settle a few facts about your business, so the site and Google say the same thing everywhere.
  • Search Console is how Google tells the owner what it found, what is broken, and what people searched to find them.
  • The Business Profile is the map listing and the panel on the right of Google results. For a local business it brings more calls than the website does.
  • Reviews on the Business Profile are the strongest local ranking signal a small business controls.
  • Bing feeds Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and several AI answer engines. Importing from Search Console takes two minutes.
  • Plan the move of the site to your own domain.

Questions we will ask

No need to answer in advance. Have a think, and if you are not sure, we will work it out together.

  • Do you want a phone number on the site? If yes, which number, and is it OK for texts?
  • Which email should enquiries go to?
  • How should people book: a calendar link, a call, a text, or an email?
  • What are your session prices (or should prices stay off the site for now)?
  • Which hours or days do you take sessions, or is it by appointment only?

  • systeme.io (to export your email list before the domain moves)
  • Your phone with the camera, near your workspace or your tools: Google may ask for a short video to confirm the business is real.
