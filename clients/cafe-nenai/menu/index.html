Our Menu

Taste a bit of South America

Gladys Benitez and Elena Sanguinetti have created a menu using their Latin American background. Cafe Nena'i has a come-and-go service throughout for food and drinks. "Taking inspirations from around the world, all the delicacies on the menu are foods we love to make, but most importantly foods we like to eat."

Our menu is designed to allow people to come in and taste a bit of South America. We have mostly foods from Paraguay and Argentina, but we also serve specialty Cuban espresso drinks and Venezuelan arepas. We have fresh juices and we like to test out new flavors once in a while.
