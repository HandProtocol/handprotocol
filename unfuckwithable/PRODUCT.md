# Unfuckable With (Courtney) · PRODUCT.md

register: brand

## Product purpose
A small course-sales site for Courtney's brand **Unfuckable With** (project slug: unfuckwithable). Four pages: a cover page, an about page, and one sales page per course. The site is a design preview for Courtney; the real site is built on Kajabi later, using these pages as the reference (Kajabi's checkout stays the checkout).

## Offers
- **The Energetic Reset** (mini-course, $167 USD): protect, clear and recover your energy in a few minutes a day. Seven lessons. Full copy exists (her words).
- **Becoming Unfuckwithable** (main course, price and contents to come): the full path. Copy is placeholder until Courtney supplies it.

## Users
People who feel everything: empaths, healers, coaches, facilitators, healthcare workers, therapists, anyone who works closely with other people and ends the day drained. They arrive mostly from Instagram on a phone. They are tired of being run by other people's moods and want something practical, not another crystal.

## Brand voice
Direct, warm, a little profane (the name), spiritual and practical at once. Second person. Short sentences. Her own phrases: "Stop absorbing everyone else's sh*t", "Energy must be moved with intention and specific techniques", "Feel recharged and revitalized everyday". No em dashes.

## Palette (strict)
Light gold, black, rose pink, white. Nothing else. Her Giza photo's sandstone counts as gold.

## Anti-references
Generic coach-funnel pages (countdown timers, fake scarcity, stacked testimonials with stars), wellness-beige with sage green, neon "spiritual" gradients, crystal clip art.

## Strategic principles
1. Direct: few sections, one ask per page.
2. The photo is the brand: one image, framed like a portal (oval) on every page.
3. Quiet luxury after the reset: warm white, gold hairlines, calm motion.
4. Portable to Kajabi: static HTML + one stylesheet, no JavaScript needed.
