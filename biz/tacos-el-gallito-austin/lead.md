> Tacos El Gallito is a popular taco truck located at the corner of Barton Springs and South Lamar in Austin, Texas. Known for its late-night hours, it stays open until 2:30 AM on weekdays and 4AM on weekends. The standout item on their menu is the al pastor, cooked on a trompo (a vertical spit) and served on homemade tortillas. In addition to tacos, the menu includes burritos, tortas, and quesadillas. The taco truck creates a lively atmosphere with music and a self-serve salsa bar, making it a favorite spot for late-night eats in Austin. Possibly the best Al pastor tacos we've had in Austin
