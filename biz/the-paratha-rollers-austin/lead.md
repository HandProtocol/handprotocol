> Hands down, this is the best place in Austin for paratha, and that’s not an exaggeration. If you’re craving authentic, hearty Indian street-style food, this food truck absolutely delivers.
> 
> We ordered the aloo paratha and gobhi paratha, and both were outstanding. The parathas are huge, generously stuffed, and cooked perfectly—crispy on the outside, soft inside, and packed with flavor. You can tell they don’t skimp on the filling. Every bite was rich, comforting, and genuinely delicious. Each paratha comes with chola, raita, achaar, and onions, which really completes the experience. The chola deserves special mention—it’s easily the best you can get in Austin. Well-spiced, balanced, and cooked just right, not overly oily or bland.
> 
> We also tried a few chaat items. The raj kachori was decent but not the highlight for me—probably a 2 out of 5 compared to the rest of the menu. On the other hand, the sabudana vada was very good: crisp on the outside, soft inside, and paired with an excellent chutney that really elevated the dish.
> 
> The seating area is cozy and comfortable, which is a nice bonus for a food truck setup. It feels welcoming rather than rushed. The chai was very good as well—strong, flavorful, and you can even get refills, which is rare and appreciated.
> 
> Overall, this place is a must-visit for paratha lovers. Great portions, authentic taste, and solid value. I’ll definitely be back. Also, pure vegetarian!!!
