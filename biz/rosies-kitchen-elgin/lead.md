> One of my first interests, when I visit a new place, is to find one of those hole-in-the-wall restaurants that the locals like to visit. Rosie's is one of those finds. If you're going thru Elgin, TX, at around lunchtime and are in the mood for some Chinese-Vietnamese food, this place should be on your radar.
> 
> Rosie's is a food truck, planted at the corner of a gas station and open only on Mon-Wed-Fri for lunch (because Rosie reserves the other days for taking care of her elderly parents). The menu is not very extensive but whatever is there is very tasty. All food is delivered in take-home containers but there's a table with four chairs there, if you want to take advantage of a nice day. For someone like me, a regular build 5'10" guy, there's enough quantity to make two meals for me... and the price doesn't even cross into double digit dollars!
> 
> If you're short of time, you can even order ahead and pick up the food when you arrive. The phone number is in one of the pics.
