> This is a new little Salvadorian food truck that popped up recently in my neighborhood. It's actually behind my neighborhood on 183 right across from the airport. I was excited when I saw it was a pupusa food truck!
> 
> Turns out the lady does a slight mix of central american food to include tacos, enchiladas and empanadas and even aguas frescas. Everything appears to be pretty much from scratch except her french fries, which is fine. Just have her make them crispy if you don't like softish steak fries. On my first visit I tried a mix of her grub and everything was pretty good. The stars of the show however, were her freshly made, piping hot pupusas. The pupusas themselves are crispy and chewy as well as super cheesy and just plain delicious! My favorite is the chicharrón and cheese, (pupusa chicharrón is just finely minced pulled pork). However the cheese and loroco, (edible flower) is also delicious. The mixta with beans, chicharrón and cheese is not to be left behind though! I went back today and just got one of each because I couldn't decide! LOL. As for the dipping sauces, I asked for both the spicy green and the mild red to try them both, and darn if it was hard to decide which one to dip my bites into. Both are super tasty. The tomato one is soft and perfectly compliments the pupusa flavors. The green is tangy and spicy and totally changes the direction of the pupusa flavors with a kick.
