> Soleil Weeks, 5/5

> After having been recommended Mimi's ages ago, decided to give it a try today. You can smell the stir fry in the wok when you walk up. Noticed hair nets on those working and was very happy to see it. The one taking orders was very friendly and helpful, answered my questions about time for wait and how the food like lo mein comes. Paid a little over $37 for 2 orange chicken plates with fried rice and a veggie lo mein plate plus 2 chicken egg rolls. Wait was estimated 15 minutes and only took 8 to get the food brought over to us. Half litre Mexican Cokes are $4 a piece plus tax but honestly worth it. They accept cash and cards including tap pay and you can get your printed receipt automatically or extra bags if you ask for them. The lo mein is cooked a bit more for those who want their noodle al dente, these are cooked a bit past that, but are not falling apart. The veggies have a little crunch left to them which I appreciate for the different textures in the meal. The orange chicken has a delicious sauce and a bed of shredded cabbage and carrots, plus there's three hot peppers and what tasted like some seared candied orange peel in with it. The rice is fried heavily, so it could be a bit dry, but mix it with your meats and sauce and it'll be just fine. Honestly for $12-$18 per person it's a great value and spot to get a hearty lunch. Definitely a huge enough serving size that there'll be some leftovers. The plate comes with what looks like a fried spring roll filled with rice noodle and cabbage, and the egg roll I purchased on the side was very crispy but juicy on the inside. I'd definitely recommend Mimi's for anyone around the area, I know I'll have to stop here again.  Oh and for those who dine in! There are 4 well sized picnic tables under a secure awning, so you can sit and be in the shade.
