## Reviews

> Just moved here to Elgin about 2 years now, tried many of the Mexican food establishments, with no regrets! A friend of mine told me about this place, Dos Amigos, and I told him I seen it but just drive by. Well yesterday I went out to run errands and was again driving by, then decided to try it. There was a line of people and about 8 cars in the parking lot. First I only ordered 1 fajita taco, just to give it a go. OMG! It was amazing! Meat was tender, flavorful, and the salsa, hit the spot! Well I had to prefer more! 2 beef and 2 chicken tacos later, my belly was satisfied! Well I’m back for more today, Sunday. This time I had the pork carnitas! Even better! So if you are ever in Elgin, TX check out this place on the weekends! They will not fail you! Enjoy!
