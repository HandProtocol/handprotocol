> I had a wonderful experience. The lady taking my order didn't know English very well, but she did understand enough to answer all of my questions. Her young daughter was also working with her and could help with any issues that needed better translation. I was recommended the Torta Costra with bistek. I also ordered an egg & sausage breakfast taco. I was a little surprised when I was charged $2 on top of my order as a credit card fee, but such is business I guess. And it's a small fee to pay to support local and to help out this lovely family.
> 
> Also, the credit card machine didn't give me the option to tip, which I was bummed about. Because no one ever has cash and I wish I could have tipped them more than I was able to.
> 
> The food was served with an avocado salsa that reminded me a lot of the green sauce from Taco Deli. Almost like they reverse engineered it and now have the recipe themselves. Delicious and a small, well-balanced kick. The breakfast taco was nice and moist. Great flavor and the egg wasn't too dry nor too soggy. Dunno if it's my favorite one in town, but it's a real solid choice. Maybe add a nopales option and I'll revisit this topic :) I wasn't raised with this kind of food, so I don't know how traditional the food was. But I have had my fair share of tortas and I think this is the first one that had a hot dog in it. That said, I absolutely didn't mind, because the texture and flavors were so well-balanced that I absolutely devoured that meal.
> 
> My only issues (and these really are so minor) were with the atmosphere. One part was the location. I don't mind it myself, because this food truck is literally 3 minutes from my house-- and I totally understand that starting a business, you can't always pick your ideal locations. But the atmosphere took a little hit being in a dusty lot surrounded by a bunch of U-Hauls and a Tire business. I would feel compelled to explain the atmosphere before we got there, but also, if that was a deterring issue, I'd probably stop talking to the person I was trying to take. This place really is really a hidden gem and I honestly feel bad that their food truck might not be very visible in the location they're in. They were so great! But they really are tucked away in a place I can only imagine doesn't get much business. My complaints aside, they do have a pretty cozy outdoor seating area. And more seating that I usually see for food trucks, so that was also nice.
> 
> However, the other atmospheric issue was how dirty/dusty the picnic tables were. Again, it's outside in a dusty area, so what are you going to do? But when I picked up my napkin, the part that was touching the table had a some visible dirt on it. A little dirt never hurt anybody, but it is a little more inconvenient if it's caked onto the table. Considering this, it would also be nice if they had one of those big jugs of hand sanitizer. Didn't really see a place to wash my hands and if the dust bothered me enough, I could at least wipe it down myself with some sanitizer.
> 
> That said, I had a lovely experience with the owner and the food was really fantastic. 10/10. I think their current 4.8 is well-deserved.
