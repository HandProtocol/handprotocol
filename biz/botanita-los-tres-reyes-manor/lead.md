> Once Upon A Taco, 5/5

> Tucked away beside a gas station off 290 sits the best little food truck in Manor.
> 
> The big blue sky stretches above and a little tuft of artificial grass protects you from the elements below as you wait—but not too long—for delicious breakfast tacos.
> 
> I've lived in Manor for two years now, and my dining experience here has been decent. Preferring instead to drive into Austin, I haven't spent as much time exploring Manor as I should.
> 
> Here at Botanita Los Tres Reyes I was surprised and delighted at every step I took. First, the pleasant look itself. The owners truly care about their little oasis. Though all around them is rough gravel, in front of them is a busy highway, behind them is barren land, this was a small pleasant area where I could enjoy the cooler weather as I awaited my food.
> 
> Most important to me was the friendly young lady who greeted me when I approached. I won't mention where but I had come from another food truck that did not even acknowledge me for quite some time, so I left. Botanita's greeted me with a welcoming smile and a "how do ya do?" Despite being much busier than this other spot.
> 
> After ordering the tacos I was surprised once more that there was no place for me to leave a tip. She took my credit card, swiped it and handed it back. Of all the times these days I am asked to tip, the one time I really want to do so, and I'm not provided any way I can. (this would be my sole complaint!)
> 
> Five breakfast tacos for $16. I would have paid more just for the "how do ya do?"
> 
> As I sat there, I further appreciated that this place was slightly behind the other food trucks. It is almost hidden.
> 
> When my order was called, I was again delighted to see how much care went into labeling and placing the tacos. Other food trucks in Manor had thrown a bunch of hastily wrapped tacos into a plastic bag, not labeling anything. So we have had to just open each one up to discover what was what. Here at Botanita's, they care.
> 
> The final delight were the tacos themselves, packed with taste and plenty of meat on a perfectly cooked tortilla.
> 
> Come to Botanita's for the tacos, stay for the "How do ya dos!"
>

> Yasselin Sanz, 5/5

> Been living in Manor since 2017, and I can count on one hand how many spots that are actually good to eat at in Manor. I can now say Botanita Los Tres Reyes has just made my top 5! Came today for a late lunch at 2pm.
> 
> I tried the sopes and they were some of the best I have ever had! They were cooked to perfection. I tried one cecina and one beef fajita. I wish they had a littttle more meat, I ran out with a good amount of masa left. I also tried their guacamole, it is traditional.
> 
> Blanca runs the truck and is the sweetest woman ever. She is very friendly and takes her time explaining things to you if you have questions. She let me sample her agua frescas, and I went with the cantaloupe. It was SO fresh, I am so excited to try their other flavors. She rotates her aqua frescas. Horchata is available in the morning up until they sell out. They also do mango, hibiscus, pineapple. Also, she can make a michelada mix without the beer, and you can just grab one next door, if you want. Can't wait to try that! She does make a topo chico one as well. On Sundays they offer shrimp cocktail, menudo, and carnitas.
> 
> Plenty of parking all around the truck in the gravel. They are located right behind the Twice the Ice business. They have 3 benches located in both shade and sun to sit at. You can call ahead to order to go as well.
