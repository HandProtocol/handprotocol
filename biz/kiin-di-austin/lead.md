> janna k, 5/5

> TL;DR: Will be coming back and would highly recommend to a friend!
> 
> Food:
> My friends and I ordered the Thai Me Down, Khao Soi, The Lost Recipe, and Talay Prik Klua.
> + The Thai Me Down is a really good starter -- it has a good portion of meat and flavor variation with both spiciness and sweetness. At a lot of Thai restaurants I go to, the meat ends up being a bit dry, but 'twas not the case here! The outside is crispy while the inside is nice and juicy.
> + I would totally order the Khao Sao again! The chicken is so incredibly tender, and soaks so much of the coconut curry flavor. I appreciate that this dish isn't very spicy yet still carries a lot of distinguishable flavor to it.
> + I got to try some of The Lost Recipe and thought it tasted pretty similar to the Thai Me Down. The fried chicken is seriously so good crispiness-wise while not drying out the chicken. However, for the best bang for your buck, I would recommend getting the Thai Me Down and something with more variation for the main dish.
> + Okay WOW the Talay Prik Klua is *spicy*. A few bites of this had me sweating bullets and drinking an absurd amount of water (I have a pretty low spice tolerance tbf). Aside from the spice, the squid and shrimp are fried and taste AMAZING. I would order this again next time if my spice tolerance was higher for sure.
> 
> Service:
> All of the staff here seem so friendly, and they checked in on our table multiple times during the service. We got water refills without asking, and they were kind enough to bring me a stack of napkins because I was sweating from the spice so much. This made my experience here so much better.
> 
> Ambiance:
