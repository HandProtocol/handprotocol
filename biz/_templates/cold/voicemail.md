A 25-second voicemail for when the call goes unanswered. Read it aloud and time it before standardizing; small business voicemail boxes often cut off at 30 seconds.

**REP:** Hi [FIRST_NAME], this is Russell with HAND Protocol, an Austin organization that builds websites for small businesses and routes 33% minimum into a pool that funds free web work for nonprofits. We're under a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor, so that portion is tax-deductible. I noticed your site for [BUSINESS_NAME] and had a quick idea. You can reach me at [PHONE] or [EMAIL]. Thanks.
