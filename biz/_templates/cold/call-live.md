Written for the operator to read aloud, and for the NotebookLM audio pass. The two-speaker exchange below shows the natural rhythm; the operator's lines stand alone as a monologue if the prospect doesn't engage.

**REP:** Hi, is this [FIRST_NAME]? This is Russell with HAND Protocol. Got a minute?

**PROSPECT:** What's this about?

**REP:** Real quick. HAND is a small organization in Austin that helps nonprofits and impact entrepreneurs get websites built, mostly funded by grants and contributors. When we work with a small business like [BUSINESS_NAME], 33% minimum of what you pay routes back into the pool that funds the free work for the nonprofit side. We're under a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor, so that portion is tax-deductible if you'd like a receipt.

**REP:** I noticed your site [is missing / could use a refresh] and figured I'd reach out. Mind if I send a short proposal, or do you have 10 minutes this week for a quick call?
