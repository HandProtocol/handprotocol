> I had been wanting to visit Santa Barabacha for a while because so many people recommended it and I had read great reviews. I’m happy to say it did NOT disappoint — I absolutely loved it!
> 
> First of all, the location is very pleasant and the overall vibe is amazing. It’s lively and welcoming, with a great mix of people — young crowds, couples, families — everyone feels welcome. There’s plenty of seating, and the space itself is very comfortable and enjoyable.
> 
> Right next door there’s a coffee bar where you can grab a drink, and they also have a cocktail bar with really good cocktails. Everything about the setup feels well thought out.
> 
> Now let’s talk about the food — the barbacoa is delicious! I ordered a barbacoa taco and it was incredible. They serve the tacos on different types (and even colors) of tortillas, which makes it even more fun. I highly recommend the quesadilla — it’s huge, filled with cheese and barbacoa, and phenomenal. It’s packed with so much meat that you can literally feel the weight of it when you lift it. Every bite is so satisfying.
> 
> The chilaquiles are also amazing. They come with a fried egg, avocado, plenty of barbacoa, and perfectly seasoned chips underneath. They have a slight kick — maybe just a little spicy — but in my opinion, they’re perfectly balanced.
> 
> And don’t leave without trying the strawberry horchata — so good!
> 
> Overall, I give this place a 10/10. Great atmosphere, fantastic food, and an excellent experience. I highly recommend coming here with family or friends — you won’t regret it!
> 
> * One small note  because it does get very busy, make sure to double-check your order before you leave. They had a big crowd when I went, and my order was missing two tacos. I didn’t go back since the line was long and the wait was already a bit much, so I just let it go.
> 
> Just a friendly heads-up to review your order before walking away, especially during peak hours!
>
