> Ryan, 5/5

> This is the most honest and real review you will read about this location. I’m not speaking out of impulse: I went 4 times to eat so I could give a fair opinion… and this is what I experienced.
> 
> I truly wanted to love this place. I tried it on four different occasions, spending over $30 for two people each time, hoping to feel that taste of home we seek when we miss Venezuelan food. Unfortunately, I left with a full stomach… and an empty heart.
> 
> Service:
> Even before the self-order kiosk, customer service was poor: no greeting, no “good morning,” no “welcome.” With the kiosk, it became worse. There is no human interaction at all. They hand empanadas and pastelitos together without labeling or explaining which flavor is which.
> 
> Food:
> Empanadas (almost $4 each): yes, they are crunchy due to pilado corn dough, but not in a good way. They are over-fried, hard, and very greasy, which aligns with other reviews calling them “stiff empanadas.”
> Pastelitos (almost $3 and very small): several came overly fried. The ham and cheese pastelito had one slice of ham and one slice of cheese, and the cheese wasn’t even melted.
> Meats: repeatedly over-salted.
> Garlic sauce: thick with herbs (possibly parsley), but extremely salty and unpleasant. It does not represent authentic Venezuelan garlic sauce.
> Tequeños: taste like frozen supermarket products. Sad that this is the most acceptable item.
> 
> Price vs satisfaction:
> I have spent over $30 here more than once, and I never left satisfied. I make better empanadas and pastelitos at home, with more love and better flavor. This place doesn’t satisfy a Venezuelan craving — it harms it.
> 
> Identity and culture:
> I am not from Zulia, I am from eastern Venezuela. But if a business carries the name “Tierra Zuliana,” it must honor it. This food does not represent our roots, nor bring pride… it brings sadness.
> 
> I am giving it 2 stars because this business could still be saved if it actually listens to reviews and cares enough to improve. But to be honest, after four attempts, I would not return.
>
