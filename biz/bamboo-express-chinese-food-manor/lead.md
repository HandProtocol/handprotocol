> I’m very picky about the Chinese food I eat. I stumbled across this cute Chinese food truck here in Manor, TX one day and got curious to try them out. Last night I ordered a serving of Shrimp Fried Rice and Beef Lo-Mein. The staff was courteous and answered all of my questions about their menu. Mind you I showed up thirty minutes before closing. They only have one size for everything, so one plate of Shrimp Fried Rice came to $16.50 and a plate of Beef Lo-Mein came to $16.25 without tax. The servings are huge in portion, I was a bit overwhelmed because it was only for me and nobody else. However the food is prepared fresh and hot off the pan. The food tastes great the only thing is the shrimp on the Fried Rice, maybe don’t put breading on the shrimp and leave it as is. It was a bit strange to me because all the Shrimp Fried Rice I’ve eaten the shrimp is cooked without any breading on it. Plus the steam from the rice makes the breading on the shrimp a bit soggy and fall/break off. Just leave the shrimp bare but other than that everything was great. I was stuffed with just one plate from both menu items. I’ll try to eat some more leftovers but I fear I got so much I won’t be able to finish it all. :)
