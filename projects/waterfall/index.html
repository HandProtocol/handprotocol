the source · governance

Built in the open

HAND Protocol is becoming a nonprofit foundation in Austin, Texas — and every document it will operate by was published before incorporation: Articles, Bylaws, board policies, a theory of change, the Form 1023 narrative. Working drafts anyone can read, comment on, and hold us to.

The pool is the primary mechanism. Contributors and Reciprocates match through three flows — Donate, Exchange, Receive — under bylaws that govern the resource pool itself (Article XI) and an optional, community-owned AI layer for the groups who choose it (Article XII).

the cycle · reciprocity

Two sides of one fabric

Supporting HAND and being supported by HAND are the same gesture in two directions. There is no applicant lane — Contributors pour skills, tools and funds into a shared pool, and Reciprocates draw from it: web presence, branding, formation paperwork, and the long arc of staying involved.

Like this water — what falls, pools; what pools, rises; what rises, returns. No one is only giving, and no one is only receiving.

the tools · technology

Leading & experimental, owned by the community

HAND builds with leading and experimental AI — and hands the keys over. Sovereign Reciprocates are custom open-source agent systems built per community, owned by that community outright, governed by eight sovereignty principles.

The same hands build the web: handmade sites for small businesses, bilingual by default, delivered free as working demos before anyone signs anything. This waterfall is drawn live by code too — no video, no textures, only light.
