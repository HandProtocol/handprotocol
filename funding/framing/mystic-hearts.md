# Mystic Hearts: Framing for HAND Audio and Public Copy

*Source document for NotebookLM and other generated material. Founder-approved framing for how Mystic Hearts is described to external audiences. Do not lead with tech stack details. Lead with the IRL human reality.*

## What Mystic Hearts is

Mystic Hearts is a **healing land that encourages growing a collective that heals**.

It is not a software product. It is not a SaaS platform. It is a real place that hosts real events where real practitioners teach real healing functions to real people who attend in person. The land and the collective are the thing. Everything else is in service to them.

The events are anonymous by design. People attend, learn, and grow without their participation being indexed, tracked, or made visible by default. Anonymity is not a privacy feature; it is a constitutive part of what makes this kind of healing work.

The healing modalities taught at Mystic Hearts events sit in the lineage of somatic and contemplative practice: breathwork, sound work, energy work, and the deeper traditions those practices come from. Practitioners hold space for these functions to be learned and integrated. Attendees become part of the collective whose growth is the point.

## HAND's role

HAND provides **the backend that helps Mystic Hearts grow**. Not the tech in front of attendees. The basic infrastructure underneath: enough structure for events to run, for the collective to expand, for practitioners to coordinate, while preserving the anonymity and in-person nature of what happens on the land.

This is the pattern HAND intends to replicate across other Reciprocate communities, identified through HAND's ongoing local discovery work. Mystic Hearts is the first and, as of 2026-05-19, the only confirmed one. Other communities are research, not roadmap. Each future community would get a backend tuned to its particular shape, owned by the community it serves — but specific second communities are not named publicly until they are real partners.

## Voice and tone rules

- **Lead with the events, the land, and the collective.** Not the platform.
- **Name the modalities by tradition, not by trademark.** Breathwork, sound, energy work, somatic practice, contemplative practice.
- **Anonymity is a feature of the gathering, not a checkbox in software.** Talk about it as a quality of the experience.
- **HAND's backend is invisible service.** Mention it briefly as supporting infrastructure. Do not list features, frameworks, or tech.
- **The audience is the curious general listener, the prospective Reciprocate, and the grantmaker who funds community-rooted healing work.** Not a venture investor evaluating product-market fit.

## What to avoid

- Phrases like "Mystic Hearts is a Next.js platform", "the app", "the product", "feature list", "user flow", "booking system as a service".
- Implying that Mystic Hearts will scale by adding software features. Mystic Hearts scales by the collective growing.
- Naming specific substances or controlled practices. The framing should signal the territory without flagging anything that narrows the audience or invites mischaracterization.
- Treating attendees as users, customers, or members in a transactional sense. They are people in a community.
