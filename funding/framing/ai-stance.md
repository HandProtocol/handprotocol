# HAND's AI Stance: Bleeding Edge and Sovereign

*Source document for NotebookLM and other generated material. Founder-approved framing for how HAND's AI workstream is described to external audiences. Overrides any specific-model references that appear in older source documents.*

## The position

HAND's AI stance is **bleeding edge and sovereign**. Both at once. Neither alone.

**Bleeding edge** means HAND uses whatever models do the job best. Leading frontier models when capability matters most. Experimental and open-source models when sovereignty, customization, or specialization matters more. The choice is made per task, per Reciprocate group, per moment in a rapidly moving landscape. There is no fixed allegiance to any specific model or vendor.

**Sovereign** means HAND maintains the right to leave any of them. No vendor lock. No proprietary single-path dependency between a Reciprocate group and their own system. The eight sovereignty principles govern this: open methodology, group-owned adapters, self-hostable end-to-end, revocable consent, full audit trail, case-by-case weights publication, no cross-group data extraction, and the open option always live.

The two are deliberately held in tension. Bleeding edge without sovereignty is vendor capture in fast fashion. Sovereignty without bleeding edge is principled stagnation. HAND is both.

## What this rules out in audio and copy

- **Do not name specific open-source base models** (e.g., "Llama 3.1 8B or Mistral 7B"). The actual choice rotates as the landscape shifts. Naming a fixed pair dates the work and misrepresents the practice.
- **Do not name specific frontier APIs as the long-term path** (e.g., "we run on Claude" or "GPT-powered"). They are tools used in evaluation and burst capacity. They are not the home.
- **Do not flatten the stance to "open-source only" or "best models only."** The political position is the combination.

## What this affirms

- HAND uses leading frontier models where capability is decisive.
- HAND uses experimental and open-source models where sovereignty, customization, and the long-term home of each Reciprocate group's system matter most.
- HAND's posture is forward and unsentimental about which model class wins which task this quarter.
- HAND's commitment is that whichever model is in the loop today, the Reciprocate group can leave with their work tomorrow.

## Voice rules

- **"Leading and experimental"** is the standard pairing. Use both.
- **"Bleeding edge and sovereign"** is the political phrasing when the stance itself is the topic.
- **Open source** is part of the answer, not the whole answer.
- Specific model names (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Qwen, Mixtral, anything else) appear in engineering documentation, not in mission audio, grant narrative, or public copy.

## Why this exists

The earlier source documents (`AI-RECIPROCATES.md`, `AI-EVAL-FRAMEWORK.md`) name specific open-base models as the planned choice. A NotebookLM audio on 2026-05-18 echoed those names and produced a framing that was technically narrow and politically too quiet. Founder correction on 2026-05-19: the actual practice is broader, and the political stance is louder.

This document is the canonical framing going forward. The older docs will be edited in a coordinated pass; until then, this overrides.
