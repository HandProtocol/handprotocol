Episode one of a three-part mission brief on HAND Protocol, a 501(c)(3) in formation in Austin, Texas. This episode answers: why does HAND exist?

Two-host conversational tone, warm and evidence-first. Lead with the human problem: regenerative impact workers (healers, grassroots organizers, social entrepreneurs) are doing essential work without sustainable resource pools. Cover the pivot from blockchain-first to human-centered. Explain the pool-of-resources skill-exchange model. Name the three communities of regenerative impact work. Reference the discovery research on historical mutual-aid failures as the negative space the model fills.

Use the words Reciprocates and Contributors as the two halves of the model. Skip the org-chart stuff (that is episode two). Skip the product list (that is episode three). End by setting up what comes next: how the foundation is structured to avoid the failures it studied.

Target length: ten minutes.
