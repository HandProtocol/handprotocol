# HAND Protocol, Week of 2026-05-18

*Update covering the seven days ending 2026-05-18. 26 commits across the public site, the governance package, the funding pipeline, and a small 3D visualization side-quest.*

## Headline

HAND's 501(c)(3) machine came online this week: a complete governance package landed in the repo, the first grant went out the door to Arcee, and the vocabulary stabilized with Companions renamed to Reciprocates across the entire project.

## What shipped

- **The public website went live at handprotocol.org.** The campaign page, the discovery research surfaces, the legacy section, and a top-to-bottom design audit pass all landed in a single push, the largest commit of the project to date.
- **A full governance package.** Sixty-five files in one commit: bylaws, articles of incorporation, fourteen policies (conflict of interest, gift acceptance, document retention, executive compensation, whistleblower, data sovereignty and AI, privacy, equal opportunity, financial management, code of ethics, community standards, fiscal sponsorship, volunteer, grant management), a board kit (composition matrix, officer job descriptions, member agreement, succession plan, prospect brief, outreach templates, annual disclosure), a program kit (theory of change, logic model, 2026 to 2028 strategic plan, Reciprocate selection and graduation), and the Form 1023 narrative draft.
- **A PDF kit builder.** Six printable bundles, from the filing kit through the policy book and program kit, so the same source markdown ships as both a public web view and review-ready PDFs.
- **A public /governance/ route.** The whole package is transparent, not buried in the repo. Future grantmakers can read it before asking.
- **Reciprocates everywhere.** The rename completed across files, folders, copy, and the public /sovereign-reciprocates/ surface. The vocabulary is now stable and the documents agree with each other.
- **A brand voice sweep.** Forty-three files cleaned of em dashes in a single pass, plus a reframe of the audience as the three communities of regenerative impact work.
- **An ASL fingerspelling loading screen.** A small piece of mission-aligned delight: a J-Toastie 3D hand fingerspelling "HAND" while the site boots, with a focused /3dhand-test/ page for tuning splay, curl, and orientation in real time.

## Governance and funding

The funding pipeline went from zero to nine entries this week.

- **Submitted:** Sovereign Reciprocates went out to Arcee AI's Trinity Builders program on 2026-05-17. The ask is tiered API credits against Arcee's Trinity-family models, valid ninety days from approval. This is the first time HAND has appeared in any funder's queue.
- **Drafting now:** Five applications opened on 2026-05-18 alone. Aspiration Tech and Hack Club Bank for fiscal sponsorship (both unlock 501(c)(3) status while the federal filing is in review), Cloudflare Tier 1 startup credits at $10,000, Netlify's OSS plan, and Vercel's $3,600 OSS credit pack.
- **Research:** A seventeen-program low-hanging-funding survey is in the repo, scoped to opportunities that either do not require 501(c)(3) status or come via a fiscal sponsor reachable in weeks instead of years.

The governance package and the funding push are the same story: the board kit, the policy book, and the 1023 narrative are exactly what fiscal sponsors and grantmakers ask for. Last week we could not have answered the questions on these forms. This week we can.

## New surfaces and projects

- **/governance/** is the most consequential. It is the public face of the package above, anchored by the rebalanced flywheel diagram that puts community and reciprocation at the center and AI in its proper place as one tool among many.
- **/discovery/** holds the landscape research: the impact organization scan, the skill-exchange-models comparison, and the vision document. These are the artifacts that justify why a pool-of-resources model belongs in this niche.
- **/sovereign-reciprocates/** is the renamed home for the AI workstream, now coherent with the rest of the language.
- **/3d-test/loading.html** and **/3dhand-test/** are a focused detour into a hand visualization that will eventually anchor the loading state across the site. Small surface, large symbolic weight: the project is called HAND.

## What's next

- **Get the 1023 narrative reviewed and filed.** The draft is in the repo. The next step is a legal pass and the federal submission, which starts the clock on official 501(c)(3) status.
- **Move the five drafting grants to submitted.** Cloudflare, Netlify, and Vercel are short-form OSS applications. Aspiration and Hack Club Bank are bigger conversations: picking a fiscal sponsor is a one-way door, and the choice depends on which funder relationships each one unlocks downstream.
- **Begin board recruitment.** The prospect brief, outreach templates, and composition matrix are ready. The next move is reaching the first two or three candidates.
