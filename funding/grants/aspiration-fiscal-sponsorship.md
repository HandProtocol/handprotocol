# Aspiration · Fiscal Sponsorship

## TL;DR

Aspiration is a fiscal sponsor with the highest mission alignment of any candidate surveyed. They explicitly serve "mission-aligned technology, data, and digital rights projects with open and equitable approaches to community, governance, and IP" — which reads like a slightly more formal version of HAND's Sovereign Reciprocates language. Full back-office support, sliding-scale fees, $150 non-refundable application fee, 30-min interview. Run as the primary track; HCB is the speed backup.

## The program

Aspiration's pitch is full-service, not minimal-overhead. They cover HR, payroll, contracts, A/P, A/R, banking, and monthly financial reporting on the sponsored project's behalf. The trade is: higher operational fee (sliding scale, industry typical 8–15%), but you don't have to build that back office yourself.

**What sets Aspiration apart:** they actively curate for digital-rights/sovereignty/open-source-public-interest framing. Sponsored projects tend to be taken seriously by digital-rights funders (Ford, MacArthur, Mozilla Foundation, NLnet, Open Technology Fund). This is a prestige tier as much as an operations tier.

**What we know:**
- Sliding scale ceiling unverified — assume industry-typical 8–15% for full-service back office.
- $150 application fee, non-refundable.
- 30-minute interview as part of intake.

## Fit assessment

**Score: 5/5.** Direct alignment:

1. **Mission language match.** Aspiration's published criteria use the exact phrases HAND uses internally: "open and equitable approaches to community, governance, and IP." Sovereign Reciprocates' eight sovereignty principles slot directly into Aspiration's worldview.
2. **Funder network unlock.** Aspiration-sponsored projects access digital-rights and tech-for-justice funders that HCB-sponsored projects rarely reach. This is the path to grants in the $25K–$250K range, not just the micro-grant tier.
3. **Operations are real.** HAND has been operating as a one-person founder show. Aspiration's full back office solves a real problem — payroll, contracts, monthly books — that we'd otherwise have to build or contract piecemeal.

## Application answers — draft

To be drafted before submission. The intake form is light; the 30-min interview is where the application actually happens.

Key talking points to prepare for the interview:

- **Sovereignty thesis.** Eight principles from the Sovereign Reciprocates docs. Group-owned AI systems, not platform-owned.
- **Why fiscal sponsorship, not direct filing.** Same answer as HCB: federal exemption in progress, near-term grants and donors gate on 501(c)(3).
- **Why Aspiration specifically over HCB or PPF.** The digital-rights funder network, the back-office support, and the mission curation. Be honest about applying to HCB in parallel — Aspiration likely respects diligence.
- **Existing artifacts to point at.** handprotocol.org (live), the governance docs, the Trinity Builders application, AI-EVAL-FRAMEWORK.md, the discovery research.

## Decision criteria for us

- **Accepted:** Use Aspiration as primary fiscal sponsor. Withdraw HCB if it's still in process; thank them for the conversation.
- **Declined:** Fall back to HCB if it accepted; otherwise PPF.
- **Held / want more info:** Provide quickly; don't single-thread.
