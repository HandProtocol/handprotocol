# Low-Hanging Funding Survey

HAND is pre-501(c)(3) (TX certificate of formation drafted, federal exemption not yet filed). Sovereign Reciprocates workstream submitted to Arcee Trinity Builders 2026-05-17. This survey targets opportunities that (a) do not require 501(c)(3) status, or (b) come via a fiscal sponsor we could realistically join in weeks not years.

## 1. AI / LLM compute credit programs

### Cohere Labs Catalyst Grants
- **Link**: https://cohere.com/research/grants (application: https://cohere.com/research/grants/application)
- **Provides**: Free Cohere API credits (no public dollar cap; "grant credit applied to Production API key")
- **Eligibility**: Academic partners, **civic and public-impact organizations** — explicitly opens door to mission-driven orgs without 501(c)(3) requirement. Project must release scientific artifacts / data-for-good outputs.
- **Effort**: Narrative form, rolling.
- **Timeline**: Unverified — appears rolling/quarterly based on Cohere Labs comms.
- **Fit**: Strong. "AI for prosocial goals in healthcare, education, social science" maps directly to Mystic Hearts + Sovereign Reciprocates. Doubles up on inference providers alongside Arcee.

### Together AI Startup Accelerator
- **Link**: https://www.together.ai/startup-accelerator (also Research Credits: https://www.together.ai/research-credits-program-request)
- **Provides**: $15K–$50K in platform credits (inference, fine-tuning, dedicated endpoints), engineering hours, GTM support.
- **Eligibility**: AI-native startups building on open-source models. Not 501(c)(3) gated. HAND can apply as the building entity behind Sovereign Reciprocates.
- **Effort**: Form + short narrative.
- **Timeline**: Selection-based, unverified turnaround.
- **Fit**: Strong. Open-source-model focus aligns with HAND's sovereignty principles; complements Arcee credits with serverless inference for Mystic Hearts experimentation.

### Modal Labs Startup Program
- **Link**: https://modal.com/startups (academic: https://modal.com/academics)
- **Provides**: Up to ~$25K–$50K in compute credits (sources disagree; treat $25K as confirmed tier, higher tier unverified), plus engineering support.
- **Eligibility**: Startup program is for companies; academic program is parallel track. No 501(c)(3) gate. One-time grant per tier.
- **Effort**: Short application form.
- **Timeline**: Unverified.
- **Fit**: Good for any agent workloads needing GPU bursts (training a small Sovereign Reciprocates classifier, batch jobs). Less central than inference credits.

### OpenAI Researcher Access Program
- **Link**: https://openai.com/form/researcher-access-program/
- **Provides**: Up to $1,000 in API credits, valid 12 months.
- **Eligibility**: Active affiliation with a university, research org, **or qualifying nonprofit**. "Qualifying nonprofit" likely requires verifiable status — pre-501(c)(3) HAND may not qualify unless under a fiscal sponsor. Worth testing.
- **Effort**: Low — short form. Reviewed quarterly (Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec).
- **Timeline**: Up to 4 weeks post-decision.
- **Fit**: Modest dollar value but cheap to apply. Next window: June 2026.

### Hugging Face Community GPU Grants (ZeroGPU)
- **Link**: https://huggingface.co/ (apply via Spaces discussion thread on a given Space; see examples like https://huggingface.co/spaces/amphion/singing_voice_conversion/discussions/1)
- **Provides**: Free ZeroGPU compute attached to a public Space. Part of the $10M ZeroGPU initiative.
- **Eligibility**: Open to academic and open-source projects. No 501(c)(3) gate. Project must run as a public HF Space.
- **Effort**: Very low — comment on the Space requesting grant w/ project description.
- **Timeline**: Rolling, often days.
- **Fit**: Strong for a demoable Sovereign Reciprocates Space (e.g., healer-intake triage demo). Trivial overhead.

### Cerebras Inference Free Tier (not a grant, but worth noting)
- **Link**: https://cloud.cerebras.ai/
- **Provides**: 1M tokens/day free, no credit card, immediate signup. Llama 3.3 70B, Qwen3 32B/235B, GPT-OSS 120B.
- **Eligibility**: Anyone.
- **Effort**: None — sign up.
- **Fit**: Use today. Pairs with Trinity-Large-Thinking as a redundant inference path.

## 2. Infra credits for OSS / mission-driven projects

### Vercel Open Source Program
- **Link**: https://vercel.com/open-source-program (apply: https://open-source-program.vercel.app/)
- **Provides**: $3,600 in Vercel credits (12 months) + partner credits + prioritized community support.
- **Eligibility**: Active OSS project hosted/intended on Vercel, demonstrable impact, Code of Conduct. **No 501(c)(3) required.** Quarterly cohorts.
- **Effort**: Form-based, short narrative. Winter 2026 cohort already announced.
- **Timeline**: Quarterly intake.
- **Fit**: Strong. Mystic Hearts is on Next.js — natural fit. Apply as soon as repos are public.

### Cloudflare for Startups (Tier 1)
- **Link**: https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/
- **Provides**: Tier 1 = $10K in credits (bootstrapped, no minimum funding). BOOTSTRAPPED promo code gives $5K standalone.
- **Eligibility**: Company ≤5 years, real business entity, legitimate product. **Not 501(c)(3) gated.** Avoid Gmail addresses — use @handprotocol.org.
- **Effort**: Form + company domain hygiene.
- **Timeline**: Days.
- **Fit**: Workers + R2 + Pages cover most ancillary infra (rate limiting, image hosting, edge logic) without burning Vercel/Supabase quotas.

### Netlify Open Source Plan
- **Link**: https://www.netlify.com/legal/open-source-policy/
- **Provides**: Free production deploys, monthly credits, unlimited team members for qualifying OSS projects.
- **Eligibility**: Open-source maintainers OR public-good organizations. No 501(c)(3) required. HAND already on Netlify (handprotocol.org).
- **Effort**: Low — request via support.
- **Timeline**: Days.
- **Fit**: Direct quota relief on existing deployment. File this immediately.

### Supabase (sponsorship is informal)
- **Link**: https://supabase.com/open-source
- **Provides**: Supabase sponsors maintainers and OSS projects ad-hoc — no published application portal. SupaSquad is an advocate program, not credits.
- **Eligibility**: Unverified — no formal program.
- **Effort**: Direct outreach to OSS team.
- **Fit**: Lower priority. Worth a cold email once Mystic Hearts is shipped, since Supabase is core to its stack. Don't gate planning on this.

## 3. Micro-grants and rapid-response funds

### Awesome Foundation (any chapter, esp. Austin / Conservation & Climate)
- **Link**: https://www.awesomefoundation.org/en/submissions/new (Austin: https://www.awesomefoundation.org/en/chapters/austin)
- **Provides**: $1,000, no strings attached, monthly.
- **Eligibility**: Anyone — individuals, groups, organizations. No 501(c)(3). No fees.
- **Effort**: Single short application form. Lowest overhead in this entire doc.
- **Timeline**: Monthly cycles; one project per chapter per month.
- **Fit**: Excellent. Frame Sovereign Reciprocates or Mystic Hearts onboarding tooling as "awesome." Apply across multiple chapters (Any, Austin, Conservation & Climate).

### Octant Public Goods (Epochs + ENS round)
- **Link**: https://octant.build/en (apply guidance: https://blog.octant.build/apply-for-funding-in-an-octant-epoch/)
- **Provides**: Epoch allocations + special rounds (ENS x Octant had $80K USDC matching pool). 50 ETH community fund in earlier rounds.
- **Eligibility**: Ethereum-aligned public goods. Note: Epoch 11 was the v1 finale (closed March 2026); v2 migration underway. **Verify current round status before applying** — this is the only flag I'd raise.
- **Effort**: Narrative application + KYC.
- **Timeline**: Epoch-based.
- **Fit**: Moderate. HAND pivoted away from crypto-protocol framing, but open-source AI sovereignty + public-goods framing still lands. Track v2 launch.

### Gitcoin Grants (GG25, projected Q2 2026)
- **Link**: https://grants.gitcoin.co/
- **Provides**: Quadratic-funding matching across domain allocators. GG24 distributed $1.8M across six domains.
- **Eligibility**: Open-source public goods. No 501(c)(3) required.
- **Effort**: Project profile + Domain Allocator pitch. Domain-specific.
- **Timeline**: GG25 targeted Q2 2026 — verify dates at grants.gitcoin.co (unverified launch date).
- **Fit**: Strong if a "regen / care / mutual aid" domain runs. Apply opportunistically; matching can be meaningful (low-thousands to low-tens-of-thousands).

### Internet Society Foundation — Research Grant Program
- **Link**: https://www.isocfoundation.org/grant-programme/research-grant-programme/
- **Provides**: $200K–$500K. **Application window: 7 April – 22 May 2026, 21:00 UTC** — closes within ~4 days of this doc. If we're going to swing, swing this week.
- **Eligibility**: Individuals and organizations worldwide. Focus on inclusive access, meaningful connectivity, trustworthy Internet.
- **Effort**: High — full narrative, research framing.
- **Fit**: Borderline. Sovereign Reciprocates can be framed as "trustworthy/inclusive Internet access for marginalized communities via group-owned AI." Likely a stretch this cycle; better to target 2027 with a stronger track record. **Flag for awareness; do not rush.**

### Mozilla MOSS — DO NOT PURSUE
- MOSS is on indefinite hiatus (post-2020 restructuring). Mozilla Technology Fund + Mozilla Builders are the live alternates; Builders Democracy x AI Cohort closed March 2026. Skip until next cohort announced.

## 4. Fiscal sponsors that would plausibly take HAND

### Hack Club Bank (HCB) — top recommendation for speed
- **Link**: https://hackclub.com/fiscal-sponsorship/ (apply: https://hackclub.com/fiscal-sponsorship/apply/)
- **Fee**: 7% of revenue, flat.
- **Provides**: 501(c)(3) umbrella, bank account, debit cards, automatic 990 filing, transparency-first software (HCB is itself open source).
- **Effort**: Application + intro Zoom within one weekday.
- **Mission fit**: HCB historically skews to hackers, students, and creative tech projects. HAND's open-source + AI sovereignty + grassroots framing fits the culture. Note: Hack Club's core audience is teens — confirm HCB welcomes adult-led foundations (it does host nonprofits beyond high-school clubs, but verify in intro call). **Unverified whether HAND's scope fully qualifies; intro call is the cheapest way to find out.**
- **Unlocks**: Most "must be 501(c)(3)" grant gates instantly. Patagonia Corporate Grants explicitly accepts fiscal-sponsored applicants — would become reachable.

### Players Philanthropy Fund (PPF)
- **Link**: https://ppf.org/fiscal-sponsorship/
- **Fee**: 6% of aggregate monthly deposits. One of the lowest in the industry.
- **Provides**: 501(c)(3) shelter, grant administration, donation processing.
- **Effort**: Form-based. Mission-aligned approval, not project-aligned. Less narrative than AMP/Aspiration.
- **Mission fit**: PPF is broad/secular; takes a wide range of causes. Lighter on mission curation than Allied Media. Strong if optimizing for low fees + breadth of grant access.
- **Unlocks**: Same as HCB — opens the 501(c)(3)-gated foundation grant tier.

### Aspiration
- **Link**: https://aspirationtech.org/services/fiscalsponsorship
- **Fee**: Sliding scale, unverified ceiling (industry typical 8–15% for full-service back office).
- **Provides**: Full back-office (HR, payroll, contracts, A/P, A/R, banking, monthly financials). Most comprehensive of the four.
- **Effort**: $150 non-refundable application fee + 30-min interview.
- **Mission fit**: **Highest mission alignment.** Aspiration explicitly serves "mission-aligned technology, data, and digital rights projects" with "open and equitable approaches to community, governance, and IP." That is HAND's exact language. Sovereign Reciprocates' eight sovereignty principles map to Aspiration's worldview.
- **Unlocks**: Aspiration-sponsored projects tend to be taken seriously by digital-rights and tech-policy funders (MacArthur, Ford, Mozilla Foundation, NLnet). Higher prestige tier than HCB/PPF.

### Allied Media Projects (AMP)
- **Link**: https://alliedmedia.org/about-the-sponsored-projects-program
- **Fee**: Unverified.
- **Provides**: Fiscal sponsorship + network/amplification for media, art, tech-for-justice projects.
- **Effort**: Application + curatorial review. AMP is selective on mission.
- **Mission fit**: Strong for healing + grassroots + community-tech framing. Detroit-rooted, but national in scope.
- **Application status**: Most recent confirmed cycle: Spring 2024. **Unverified whether 2026 applications are currently open** — email sponsoredprojects@alliedmedia.org before investing time in a full application.

## Recommended next 3

### 1. Apply to **Hack Club Bank (HCB)** this week
HCB is the single highest-leverage move. A 1-week-to-intake fiscal sponsor with a 7% flat fee unlocks every grant that gates on 501(c)(3) status — including Patagonia, most family foundations, and most state-level community funds — without waiting on the IRS exemption letter (which can take 6–12 months). The intro Zoom is the cheapest possible due-diligence call; even if HCB declines, the conversation will sharpen our pitch for PPF or Aspiration. If HCB is a poor culture fit (teen-skewed), pivot immediately to Aspiration as the prestige alternative.

These three together = ~$13.6K+ in confirmed infra credits ($3,600 Vercel + $10K Cloudflare + Netlify OSS quota), zero 501(c)(3) requirement, all form-based with shared narrative material (same project description, same repo links, same maintainer bios). One afternoon of writing produces three submissions. This relieves the Mystic Hearts hosting cost curve through 2027 and frees cash for legal filing fees and contract designers. The marginal cost of applying to all three vs. just one is near zero.

Cohere Catalyst explicitly funds "civic and public-impact organizations" — no 501(c)(3) gate, and the "AI for prosocial goals in healthcare" framing is a near-exact match for Mystic Hearts.
