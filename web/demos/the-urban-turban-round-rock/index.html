The Concept

Two cuisines, one kitchen

The Urban Turban makes birria tacos the way a Pakistani chef would, with the spice technique of South Asian cooking poured into Mexican street food. The fusion works because the sensibilities line up: the same warmth, the same depth, the same love of bold spice. Everything is 100% halal, and the result is birria many people swear is the best they have ever tasted.
