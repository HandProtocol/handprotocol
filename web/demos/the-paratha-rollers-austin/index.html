What's a paratha?

A paratha is a North Indian flatbread, stuffed with spiced fillings like potato, cauliflower, or fresh paneer cheese, then cooked on a hot tawa until it turns flaky and crisp. You eat it the traditional way, torn by hand and dipped into chola, raita, and tangy achaar on the side.

If you have never had one made fresh to order, this is the place to start.
