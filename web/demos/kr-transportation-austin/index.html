Who we are

Owned by Austin. Built on showing up.

K & R Transportation is a Black-owned, veteran-owned car and limousine service based in East Austin. We started with a simple idea: a ride you can count on, a driver who is glad you called, and a door held open whether you are headed to the airport at 4 a.m. or leaving a wedding at midnight.

We keep the cars clean, we know the city, and we treat your time like it matters. We are open 24 hours because the moments worth a good ride do not keep office hours.
