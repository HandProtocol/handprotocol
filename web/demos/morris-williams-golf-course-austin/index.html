The course

A real round, close to home.

Morris Williams sits just east of downtown, with wide, rolling fairways that give you room to swing and greens that reward a good roll. It plays fair for a first-timer and still has enough teeth to keep a low handicap honest.

The clubhouse is new now, with a full restaurant, a bar, and an outdoor patio looking back over the course. Come for the round, stay for a cold drink and a bite when you walk off eighteen.
