Step 1 · Verdict

Yes — use a Model C sponsor.

The triggering criterion is §1(a): funder pipeline includes near-term deadlines requiring tax-deductible recipient status. The Foundation campaign tiers are locked, the business-outreach pipeline routes into the pool, and grant entries are already in flight. Form 1023 determination runs 12–18 months; donors and foundations that need an active recipient cannot wait on the 27-month retroactive window. Plan a 12–18 month sponsorship, exit on determination.

Step 4 · Recommendation

Hack Club Bank, with PPF as the safe pivot.

Lead with HCB. If a major near-term foundation funder signals discomfort with a newer, startup-flavored sponsor, pivot to PPF — lower fee, longer track record, more legible to traditional grantmakers. Drop SGF (capacity) and FJC (slow).

Step 5 · Before signing

Verify, then commit.

None blocking, all confirmable in May 2026. The IP item is the one that bites later if skipped.

HCB intake status Confirm they accept foundation-stage 501(c)(3)-in-formation projects, not just hackathon teams. Quick form on their site.
HCB fee current at 7% Verify against the live welcome packet; rates drift.
