Women Power

Mom and daughter team

Gladys Benitez

Gladys Benitez, a lifestyle blogger and University of Texas graduate, is the mastermind behind all things marketing and branding at the cafe. She was born and raised in Miami Beach, and describes growing up there like a melting pot of cultures — particularly those of Latin America. Growing up with a love for food, salsa and traveling, she took on the challenge to open a coffee shop after learning that it was her mother's all-time dream. Gladys always wanted to build a business and made it her goal to make the cafe a fun place for all her customers. She now co-owns the cafe and lives in Austin, Texas.

Elena Sanguinetti

Elena Sanguinetti is the master behind the taste of everything in the cafe. Born and raised in Paraguay, she brings recipes straight from her motherland. At a young age she ventured to New York and finally Miami Beach. A couple more transitions led her to Austin, Texas. And now she co-owns the cafe with her daughter and shares her delicious treats in East Austin. Nena'i was her nickname growing up in Paraguay, and it continues to be representative of the cafe and all it means to be a driven little girl from any and all parts of the world.

What Nena'i Means

Nena'i in the Guaraní language means little girl. It was a nickname given to one of our founders when she was growing up in Paraguay — walking by Abuela's house, playing on the farm lands, swimming freely in the rivers and loving life fully. Nena'i is the soul of our cafe. It represents the dreams in every little girl's heart, the love of our world and the vast possibilities of anyone from anywhere and in any walks of life.

When looking for the right place to start our business, we decided to look for a small space where we could grow alongside the area. East Riverside was a bit of an untouched location and we found this cute spot with an amazing window that let in lots of good light. We wanted to make it our little project and fill it with tons of love representing the love we have for each other. My mom and I are a team and we hope to express that in all our efforts to making this cafe a success.

Gladys & Elena
