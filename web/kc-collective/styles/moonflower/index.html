The thirteen.

Thirteen moons in a year, thirteen phases, thirteen cards. Draw one and take what it says with you.

Rooted in the sacred cycles of transformation.

We honor every woman’s path as inherently creative — whether expressed through art, healing, nurturing, or simply the courage to begin again.

Here, creativity is not a talent but a birthright — the living pulse of the feminine, ever-evolving and endlessly reborn.

Within this circle, we hold space for both light and shadow — for joy and unraveling, laughter and release. We honor healing as a sacred art, trusting that what feels like an ending is often the beginning of deeper becoming.

Our offerings flow with the rhythm of life itself.

Some moments call us inward; others invite movement, creation, and play. All are guided by the same intention — to help women reconnect, realign, and remember the magic that already lives within them.

  • Inward

    Circles of reflection

    Small, held conversations. We sit together, speak honestly, and listen without trying to fix anything.

  • Movement

    Social meetups and soulful outings

    Coffee mornings, long walks, gallery afternoons, picnics. The easy, lighthearted ways we find each other.

  • Creation

    Workshops for mind and spirit

    Journaling, gratitude practice, guided meditation, Akashic work. Tools you take home and keep.

  • Play

    Gatherings

    Seasonal celebrations, online and in person, that weave friendship, healing, and lighthearted joy.
