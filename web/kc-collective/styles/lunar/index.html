Thirteen moons. Every phase sacred.

Rooted in the sacred cycles of transformation, we honor every woman’s path as inherently creative — whether expressed through art, healing, nurturing, or simply the courage to begin again.

Here, creativity is not a talent but a birthright — the living pulse of the feminine, ever-evolving and endlessly reborn.

Within this circle, we hold space for both light and shadow — for joy and unraveling, laughter and release. We honor healing as a sacred art, trusting that what feels like an ending is often the beginning of deeper becoming.

Some moments call us inward; others invite movement, creation, and play.

  • Inward

    Circles of reflection. Small, held conversations where we speak honestly and listen without fixing.

  • Movement

    Soulful outings. Long walks, bike rides, afternoons away from the screen and back into the body.

  • Creation

    Workshops for mind and spirit. Journaling, gratitude, meditation, Akashic practice. Tools you keep.

  • Play

    Social meetups and gatherings that weave friendship, healing, and lighthearted joy.

“The cycles of women’s lives mirror the rhythm of the Earth — each season necessary, each phase sacred.”

Clarissa Pinkola Estés
