A HAND Protocol course · Free

Build your first app with Claude Code

You don't need to be a programmer. If you can describe what you want in plain English, Claude Code can build it with you — and this course teaches you how to work with it, from installing the app to putting something real on the internet.

📚 9 modules ⏱ ~45–60 min each 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 A real human has your back 📌 Pin anything to review later

How this course works

Made to be walked through — or walked alone

Guided: a HAND human walks the course with you — on a call, in person, or over messages. They can see where you are and where you got stuck, so you never have to explain from scratch.

Self-serve: everything is written so you can do it alone, at your own pace. Your progress saves automatically. And on every lesson there's an "I'm stuck" button — press it and a real person at HAND gets a ping and reaches out.

Pin for review: next to every section there's a little pin. Not 100% sure about something? Pin it, add a note about what feels fuzzy, and it lands on your personal review list to revisit — with a human, or on your own.

Nobody builds alone. That's the whole point of HAND.
