Build and iterate

The loop

Everything in this module is one loop, run many times:

  1. Look at your app in the browser.
  2. Describe one change — specific, one thing at a time.
  3. Let Claude work — review, accept.
  4. Refresh and check. Then back to 1.

Open NOTES.md from last module — your top-5 missing things. That's today's menu. Work down it one loop at a time, and add new wishes to the list as you spot them (ask Claude to keep NOTES.md updated as you go).

Desktop superpower

Try asking Claude to open the site in its browser pane and look at it — the desktop app can show your page inside the Claude window, and Claude can see it too: "Open index.html in the browser and tell me what looks off." Now you're two sets of eyes on the same screen.

Pass 1 · Real content

Placeholder text is where first projects go to die — replace it early. Real words, real hours, real menu, your actual story. Content prompts that work:

Copy into Claude Code — then edit the details 
Replace the placeholder text in the About section with this (tidy up my grammar but keep my voice): ___paste your rough words here___

For photos: drop image files into your project folder (make a subfolder called images), then tell Claude — "I added images/tacos1.jpg and images/truck.jpg — use them in the gallery." You can also paste screenshots directly into the prompt box when you want Claude to see something.

Pass 2 · Make it feel right

You don't need design vocabulary — you need references and mood words. Claude translates:

  • "Warmer and friendlier — like a neighborhood coffee shop, not a bank."
  • "I love how stripe.com feels — calm, lots of white space. Move mine that direction."
  • "The headline font feels stiff. Show me two friendlier options."
  • "Everything's cramped. Give the sections more room to breathe."

Ask for options when you're unsure: "show me this section two ways" costs nothing and teaches you your own taste fast.

Pass 3 · The phone check

Most of your visitors will be on phones. Two ways to check without owning a device lab:

Copy into Claude Code 
Check the whole site at phone size: is anything cut off, cramped, overlapping, or too small to tap? Fix what you find and give me a short list of what changed.

And the honest test: open the site on your phone. (Quick way: put the project folder in a cloud drive, or just wait — Module 07 gives it a real URL you can open anywhere.)

When something breaks — the debugging script

Something will break. This is not a sign you're failing; it's a sign you're building. The skill is reporting it well. Claude can't see your screen (unless you show it), so "it doesn't work" gives it nothing. Use this three-line script every time:

The magic three lines

1 · What I did: "I clicked the Menu button on my phone-sized window."
2 · What I expected: "The list of links should drop down."
3 · What happened instead: "Nothing happened at all."

Level it up further:

  • Paste a screenshot of the broken thing straight into the prompt box. A picture is worth a thousand descriptions.
  • Copy any error text word-for-word, no matter how unreadable it looks to you — it's usually a map for Claude.
  • Let Claude investigate: "look at the page yourself in the browser pane and figure out why the button does nothing."

If three rounds of fixing haven't fixed it: /clear, then in a fresh session, re-describe the bug with the three-line script. And if that fails — that's precisely what the I'm stuck button is for. No heroics required.

Show a human

Before the checkpoint: show your app to one person — over a shoulder or a screenshot. Watch where they get confused; ask what they'd tap first. You'll get your next three NOTES.md items instantly. (If you're in guided mode, your HAND guide is an excellent victim.)

Checkpoint

Iteration reps

Your gallery images aren't showing. Which bug report gets it fixed fastest?

Did / expected / instead — plus a screenshot. That's a professional bug report; most people never learn it.
