Put it online

⏱ About 30 minutes 🛠 Ends with a URL you can text to your mom

What "deploying" actually means

Right now your app lives only on your computer — when your laptop sleeps, your app is unreachable. Deploying just means copying your files to a computer that's always on and connected (a server), which hands them to anyone who visits your address.

For website-shaped projects like yours, companies host this for free — genuinely free, because sites like yours cost them almost nothing to serve. We'll use Netlify, the same host handprotocol.org runs on.

Step 1 · Make a free Netlify account

  1. Go to app.netlify.com/signup.
  2. Sign up with your email (no credit card, free tier is real and permanent).
  3. If it asks onboarding questions, pick whatever's closest to "personal project" — it doesn't matter.
Why account first?

You can technically drag-and-drop without an account, but anonymous sites get locked behind a password until claimed. Making the account first skips that headache entirely.

Step 2 · The drop

This is the part people don't believe until they do it:

  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop (signed in).
  2. Open your Desktop so you can see your project folder.
  3. Drag the whole project folder onto the drop zone in the browser.
  4. Wait about fifteen seconds.

That's it. Netlify gives you a live URL (something like wonderful-otter-123abc.netlify.app) — your app is on the internet. Open the link on your phone right now. Different device, different network, same app. That's not a simulation; that's the real web, and you're on it.

You can rename the ugly random address: in Netlify, open your site → Site configuration → Change site name → pick something like rosas-tacos.netlify.app.

Step 3 · Updating your live site

Your site is a copy of your files — editing with Claude doesn't change the live version until you re-deploy. The update loop:

  1. Make changes with Claude, check them locally (the loop you know).
  2. In Netlify: open your site → Deploys tab → drag your project folder onto it again.

Yes, professionals automate this (Claude can help you set that up after graduation — ask it about "connecting a GitHub repo to Netlify"). Drag-and-drop is honest work and it's plenty for now.

A word on custom domains

Want rosastacos.com instead of .netlify.app? That's a domain — the one part of this that costs money (usually $10–20/year). It's optional, it can wait, and when you're ready, Claude can walk you through buying one and connecting it to Netlify in about twenty minutes. Don't let a domain block your launch today.

Step 4 · Share it (non-optional)

Send the link to one person who will be happy for you. If you built Track B, send it to the person it's for — and maybe have a camera ready. This step is in the course because shipping to a real audience is the actual finish line of building, and because you've earned a little celebration.

A month ago this was "I'm not technical." Today it's a URL.
