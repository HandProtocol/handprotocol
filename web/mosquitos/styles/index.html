Dress the outside.
Never touch the inside.

Eight ways to make a trap something you'd want on the land.

A mosquito only ever judges three things, all at the water's mouth: it must stay dark, open, and still (and deep). Everything else — the body, the base, the planting, the light — is ours to make lovely. Every costume below keeps the dark open mouth intact, so each one is still a working trap.

● dark water target ○ open mouth to the sky ≈ still surface ⚠ no fountains / bubblers

Every costume is still a working trap. Across all eight, the mouth stays the same: a dark, open, still pool a gravid female can't resist — only the body and base change. The lift-out black bucket inside is identical; the dress is the only variable. Decorate freely, refresh the ½ dunk on the 30-day cadence, and the prettiest one works exactly as well as the plainest.
