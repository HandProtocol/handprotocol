    How to look at these

    1. Scroll each one all the way down. The first screen is the mood; the rest is how the site actually reads.
    2. Try it on your phone. Every card has a phone view; most of your visitors will meet you there.
    3. Pick more than one. Two or three favourites tell koH what you love — a mix is normal ("the feel of Cathedral with the warmth of Terra").
    4. Ignore the words for now. The copy is your draft and the photos are proofs; only the design is being decided here.
