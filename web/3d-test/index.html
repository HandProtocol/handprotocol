Scroll-driven sign language spike

Three approaches to the same idea: a hand that signs give, receive, together, grow as you scroll. Pick a winner.

A

Three.js

Procedural 3D hand. Real bones, real lighting.

Give

Give

A contributor leans forward. The first $22,777 of the Foundation raise is an act of giving forward, before any return is named.

Receive

Reciprocates, the impact entrepreneurs, small businesses, and organizers we serve, receive long-arc support. Not a grant cycle. A pool of resources.

Together

Three communities of regenerative impact work, coordinating in one place. The hand closes around something held in common.

Grow

What was given becomes infrastructure. Infrastructure compounds. The fund regenerates itself across multiple time horizons.

Stylized illustration. Finger joints rotate on scroll.

Give

Give

Together

Grow

Index extended upward. A sprout from a closed palm.
