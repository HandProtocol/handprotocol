About

Support the people
already doing the work.

HAND Protocol has been operating for over a year—supporting impact projects, participating in quadratic funding rounds, and building transparent accountability systems.

Now we're raising funds to establish a tax-exempt nonprofit foundation in Austin, Texas and formalize what's already working.

We focus on healers, impact entrepreneurs, and community organizations, providing branding, marketing, and one-on-one development support—without abandoning anyone once a website or campaign is finished.

This campaign funds the legal formation, operational runway, and systems required to scale that commitment responsibly and sustainably.

The Problem

Transformative work,
inadequate support.

Across Austin and beyond, three groups are doing vital community work with barriers that keep their impact invisible, unsupported, or unsustainable.

01

Healers & Practitioners

Hold space for community wellbeing but spend more time surviving than serving. Deep community trust, zero structural support.

02

Impact Entrepreneurs

Building healing-aligned products and communities, but traditional startup infrastructure doesn't fit their values, and nonprofit infrastructure doesn't fit their model.

03

Grassroots Organizations

Doing vital community work—harm reduction, mutual aid, food access, land stewardship—often more established but still under-resourced for sustained outreach.

Common barriers they all face:

Operate informally or under-resourced
Lack consistent branding and fundraising tools
Offered short-term help that disappears after delivery
Spend more time surviving than serving

Why Healers First?

Healers sit at the intersection of community trust, cultural continuity, and collective wellbeing—yet they are often the least supported structurally.

They're also the connective tissue. Healers are practitioners, they build products and communities, and they partner with impact organizations.

Austin is our proving ground. What works here becomes a model for expansion.
