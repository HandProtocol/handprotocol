How I got here.

Courtney's story, in her words: the moment she realised she was carrying everyone else's energy, what it was costing her, and what changed once she learned to move it with intention.

What I know for sure is this: you are more intuitive and empathetic than you think. You are constantly absorbing other people's energy, on Instagram, in a crowded room, in every close relationship, and taking on thoughts, emotions and beliefs that were never yours. That is the reason you feel fatigue, brain fog and anxiety you can't shake. It is also the reason nothing outside you gets to run you, once you know how to work with it.

What I believe

Three things every course I teach comes back to.

Courtney can rewrite these three, or add a fourth.

  1. Energy must be moved with intention and specific techniques.Palo santo, sage and breathwork are lovely. They are not enough to clear what you pick up in a day.
  2. Most of the thoughts you are thinking are not your own.Learning to tell which ones are yours changes every decision you make, from what you eat to who you stay with.
  3. Protection is a practice, not a personality.A few minutes a day, done every day, beats a weekend retreat you have forgotten by Tuesday.
