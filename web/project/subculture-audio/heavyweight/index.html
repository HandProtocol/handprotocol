Precision, Power, and Impact

The (Sub)culture Audio system proudly features the latest hand-crafted point source speakers from the renowned team at HENNESSEY SOUND DESIGN, known for redefining the live audio experience. Each speaker is built with cutting-edge design and premium materials to provide high-fidelity, powerful sound that captivates listeners.

The Haymaker is one of HSD's latest speaker designs, expertly engineered to deliver full range audio to the listener. Featuring four powerful 1-inch compression drivers, a 10-inch mid-range speaker, and an 18-inch subwoofer, a pair of Haymakers can be configured to either project high and mid-range frequencies for large crowds or function as full-range speakers for more intimate settings.

The Hammer is a versatile low-mid speaker designed to bridge the gap between the Battlehawk and Haymaker 18". Known for its punchy performance, the Hammer excels at filling in the critical low-mid frequencies, ensuring a seamless audio experience. It also doubles as a small-scale subwoofer, making it ideal for compact setups. A combination of 2 Hammers and 2 Haymakers can effortlessly handle events with up to a couple hundred attendees.
