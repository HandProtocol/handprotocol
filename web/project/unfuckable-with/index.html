Four ways The Energetic Reset could look.

Your words, your four colors, four different worlds. Each design lets a different color lead: black, rose pink, white, or light gold. Open any of them, scroll all the way down, try the phone view, and heart the ones that feel like you. When you're done, send your picks to koH from the bar at the bottom.

    How to look at these

    1. Scroll each one all the way down. The first screen is the mood; the rest is how the page actually reads and sells.
    2. Try it on your phone. Every card has a phone view, and most of your visitors will arrive from Instagram on a phone.
    3. Pick more than one if you like. A mix is normal ("the attitude of Rosé with the calm of Halo"). That tells koH what to build.
    4. The words are yours. Every line comes from your page copy. Only the design is being decided here.
    5. The photo is a stand-in. Your own image drops into the same spot in all four designs.
    6. This all moves to Kajabi. These previews are for choosing a direction. The buttons will point at your Kajabi checkout once you've picked.
