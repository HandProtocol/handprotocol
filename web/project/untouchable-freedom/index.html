    How to look at these

    1. Scroll each one all the way down. The first screen is the mood; the rest is how the shop actually reads.
    2. Try it on your phone. Every card has a phone view, and most of your visitors will arrive from a phone.
    3. Pick more than one. Two or three favourites tell koH what you love. A mix is normal ("the feel of Marble with the warmth of Ember").
    4. The buttons are real. Add to cart goes to your Shopify cart and the email box goes to your Shopify customer list, so your checkout keeps working whichever design you choose.
    5. The words are yours. Every line is from your current store and your journal's table of contents. Only the design is being decided here.
