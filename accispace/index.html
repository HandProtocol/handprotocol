    How to read ACCI

    A map is a question, not a verdict.

    What you are seeing

    Crash records from the City of Austin Vision Zero crash-level dataset. ACCI includes crashes coded as fatal or suspected serious injury, within the selected years. Columns combine records in nearby map cells. Their height weights a death more heavily than a serious injury.

    What the data cannot say

    Reports can be delayed, revised, incomplete, or imprecisely located. A concentration shows where harm was recorded, not why it happened or who is at fault. Recent totals are especially likely to change.

    Why this exists

    Safety data can feel abstract in a spreadsheet. This experimental view makes spatial patterns legible while keeping individual records available for inspection.
