## Performance indicators

### Flywheel-side (primary)

- Donate engagements (one-way Contributor gifts logged): no fixed target; track and report
- Exchange engagements (skill-for-skill matches): target 5 in Year 1, 15 in Year 2, 30 in Year 3
- Receive engagements (Companion onboarding): target 3 in Year 1, 7 in Year 2, 12 in Year 3
- Cross-participation rate (% of Contributors who also Receive, plus % of Companions who also Donate or Exchange): track and report
- Alumni return rate (graduated Companions who return as Contributors within 24 months): target 30% by Year 3
- Annual Flywheel Health Check published (target: yes, by end of Q4)

### Companion-side

- Number of active Companions (target: 3 in Year 1, 7 in Year 2, 12 in Year 3)
- Felt-continuity and felt-presence (5-point Likert; target: 4+ average)
- Infrastructure retention rate (target: 90% of Companion infrastructure remains in use 6 months after graduation)
- Companion-reported satisfaction (target: 8+ on 10-point net promoter)

### Contributor-side

- Number of active Contributors (target: 10 in Year 1, 25 in Year 2)
- Match success rate (Companion + Contributor both satisfied at 3 months, target: 80%)
- Contributor year-over-year retention (target: 70%)
- Contributor hours contributed (track and report; no fixed target)

### Sovereignty-side (applies only when Companion groups opt into Sovereign Companions)

- Companion-group opt-in rate (track and report; no fixed target; opt-in is the design principle)
- Time-to-first-draft on routine operational documents (target: 50% reduction with agent vs without, for opted-in groups)
- Adapter ownership records complete (target: 100% for opted-in groups)
- Portability drill passing (target: annual, 100% for opted-in groups)
- Revocation SLA met (target: ≤ 30 days, 100%)
- Closure simulation succeeded by Gate 3 (target: 100%)
- Audit logs accessible to Companion groups (target: 100%)

### Field-side

- Quarterly reports published (target: 4/year)
- Open-source releases (target: agent scaffolding, eval harness, methodology by Year 2)
- Citations of HAND's methodology by adjacent organizations (target: 3 by Year 3)
- Convening invitations and field presence (target: present at 4 sector convenings per year by Year 2)

### Organizational

- IRS 501(c)(3) Determination Letter received (Year 1 or early Year 2)
- Public support test passing (annual)
- Diversified funding (no single funder > 40% of revenue by Year 3)
- Compensation ratio within Board-set limit (annual)
- 100% board giving (annual)

## Assumptions

The Logic Model assumes:

a. **Capacity-building gap is real and stable.** Bridgespan's research, NCN's surveys, and HAND's discovery work all document the gap. Assumed durable for the planning horizon.

b. **Trust-based philanthropy continues to grow.** MacKenzie Scott, Kataly, Solidaire, Borealis trajectories support this; political and economic disruption is a risk to test.

c. **AI-for-social-good funding continues.** McGovern, Mozilla, Ford trajectories support this; an AI funding correction is possible.

d. **Companion groups want long-term relationships.** Discovery interviews support; first-cohort experience will validate.

e. **Open-source norms in this space.** GitHub-norm collaboration possible at the level HAND operates; if community engagement doesn't materialize, the open-source bet is weakened.

## External factors

Factors HAND does not control but that affect the model:

- Federal funding for harm reduction and mutual aid (affects Companion segment).
- State of post-accelerator impact-entrepreneur ecosystem.
- Trust-based vs. traditional philanthropy balance.
- AI-vendor pricing for inference (affects unit economics).
- Texas regulatory environment for small nonprofits.
- Generational transitions in foundation leadership.
