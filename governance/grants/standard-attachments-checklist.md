# Standard Grant Application Attachments Checklist

> The 15-item packet most foundation funders ask for. Status of each item is tracked here so HAND knows what is ready to send and what is still being prepared. Updated quarterly.

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · May 2026 · Living document

---

## How to use this checklist

Most institutional funders ask for some subset of the same 15 attachments. Maintaining each in a current, board-approved version means HAND can respond to grant inquiries within hours instead of weeks. This checklist tracks the current status of each.

The columns:

- **Item.** The standard attachment name.
- **Status.** Current readiness: Ready, In progress, Not yet applicable, or Skip.
- **Owner.** Who maintains the document.
- **Last refreshed.** When the document was last updated.
- **Where to find.** Path in the repo or shared drive.
- **Notes.** Anything funders should know.

## Production discipline

a. **Update the date stamp** at the top of each document every time it is materially revised.

b. **Version numbering** (v0.1, v0.2 ...) within each document.

c. **Maintain the differences table** between versions (most recent change at top).

d. **Public versions on website** versus working drafts in the repository: the website carries the version current as of the most recent board adoption; the repository carries drafts.

e. **Quarterly review:** the Executive Director reviews each item against this checklist quarterly and updates statuses.

## When asked for an attachment HAND doesn't have

If a funder asks for an attachment HAND does not have (typically: audited financials, organizational chart pre-staff, prior 990s pre-determination):

a. **Acknowledge the gap honestly** in the application narrative.

b. **Offer a substitute** where possible (compilation in lieu of audit; board roster in lieu of organizational chart; fiscal sponsor's 990 in lieu of own).

c. **Cite the Strategic Plan timeline** for when the requested artifact will exist.

d. **Do not fabricate or rush a substandard version** to fill the gap.

The first published Form 990 (likely 990-N for HAND's first fiscal year) is a useful early signal even though it doesn't carry financial detail. The IRS Determination Letter is the most important single document for funder confidence; until it arrives, HAND signals readiness through the governance package itself.
