# Capacity Statement

> Why HAND Protocol Foundation is ready to receive funds, deploy them well, and report on them honestly. Used as an attachment to grant applications where funders ask about organizational capacity. Honest about the fact that HAND is small and pre-track-record.

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · May 2026 · Living document

## Honest framing

HAND Protocol Foundation is a small, founder-led, pre-incorporation organization preparing to file for 501(c)(3) status in Texas in 2026. This Capacity Statement is honest about that posture. HAND is not a scaled operation with a decade of audited financials. HAND is a focused, early-stage organization with rigorous discovery work, a defensible model, and a deliberate plan to build the operating infrastructure that justifies funder trust.

This document tells you what HAND has now, what HAND is building, and how HAND will use your dollars responsibly during the build.

## What HAND has now (as of May 2026)

### The flywheel mechanism, designed and documented

- **A three-flow resource pool model** (Donate, Exchange, Receive) with worked examples (Marcus the roofer, Anya the reiki practitioner, Río Verde Collective) drawn from real conversations with the populations HAND will serve. The flywheel is the primary mechanism, designed so that everyone involved can benefit if they choose to: a single person can be both giver and receiver, and cross-participation is encouraged but never required. Documented in [`../programs/reciprocate-selection-and-graduation.md`](../programs/reciprocate-selection-and-graduation.md), [`../policies/volunteer-and-contributor.md`](../policies/volunteer-and-contributor.md), and Bylaws Article XI.

- **An alumni pathway** ensuring graduation is entry into a broader pool, not exit from a program. Graduated Reciprocates may return as Contributors, mentors, or cross-cohort connectors. The pool grows denser over time through alumni return.

- **An Annual Flywheel Health Check** required by Bylaws §11.5 and Compliance Calendar. Reports three-flow volumes, cross-participation rate, alumni return rate, and recommended program adjustments. Parallel to the Annual AI Accountability Report.

## How HAND will use your funds responsibly

### Financial controls in place from day one

- **Signing authority limits.** Single-signature ≤ $5,000; dual-signature $5,000–$25,000; dual + board resolution > $25,000.
- **Monthly bank reconciliation** by an independent reconciler.
- **Quarterly Treasurer review** of all accounts.
- **Restricted funds tracked separately** at the account and ledger level.
- **No loans to directors or officers** (prohibited by Bylaws).
- **Rebuttable-presumption procedure** in place for any executive compensation.

### Restricted-funds discipline

- Grants are recorded as temporarily restricted at receipt.
- Expenses are coded to the grant fund as incurred.
- Variance reports comparing budget to actuals run quarterly.
- Material variances (>10% on any category) require funder communication before incurring.
- Unspent funds returned per grant agreement, not retained without funder consent.

### Reporting cadence

- Monthly internal financial statements.
- Quarterly Board financial reports.
- Funder-specific reports per grant agreement.
- Annual Report (public).
- Annual Form 990 filed timely.
- Annual AI Accountability Report (public) for Sovereign Reciprocates program.

### Compliance

- The Compliance Calendar ([`../COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md`](../COMPLIANCE-CALENDAR.md)) tracks every filing, renewal, and review.
- The Document Retention Policy applies to all funder-relevant records.
- Document destruction is suspended upon any indication of audit, investigation, or litigation.

### Independent oversight

- Treasurer is an independent director, not the Executive Director.
- Finance Committee reviews monthly statements.
- Audit Committee engages external CPA for compilation in Year 1, review in Years 2-3, audit when triggered by revenue or funder requirement.
- Board approves the budget; Executive Director executes.

## What HAND will not do with your funds

- Pay for institutional rhetoric or aspirational language that misrepresents the work.
- Optimize the program ratio at the expense of the work that matters.
- Spin negative outcomes as positive.
- Co-mingle restricted and unrestricted funds.
- Engage in lobbying or political campaign activity (per Bylaws Article 4 and IRC §501(c)(3)).
- Use AI funds for non-AI work, or non-AI funds for AI infrastructure HAND has not separately disclosed.
- Train AI models on Reciprocate group data without explicit, signed consent.
- Compromise the eight Sovereign Reciprocates sovereignty principles for funder convenience.

## What's missing right now, honestly

A capacity statement that pretends to have everything is not credible. HAND has not yet:

- Filed for 501(c)(3) status (in progress).
- Received the IRS Determination Letter (expected mid-2027).
- Hired a paid Executive Director (founder is the only operational lead currently).
- Closed a full Year 1 of Reciprocate engagement and produced outcomes data.
- Engaged an external CPA (will engage at incorporation).
- Carried D&O and general liability insurance (will bind before any program activity).
- Run a full rebuttable-presumption procedure (will run before first compensation).

The strategic plan and the funder pipeline are designed to remedy each of these in a defined timeline. The Compliance Calendar tracks the dates.

## Why funder trust is well-placed here

The honest case for funding HAND:

a. **Rigorous discovery work.** The published research demonstrates that HAND has done the homework on the field and the gap.

b. **Defensible model.** Long-term relational accompaniment is hard. The unit economics challenge is real. HAND's design (Sovereign Reciprocates as parallelization, curated Contributor pool, three-year engagement) is a credible response.

c. **Sovereignty posture.** HAND's commitment to group-owned AI, open methodology, and inspectable governance is rare. It is the responsible-AI grantee profile some funders explicitly seek.

d. **Voice integrity.** HAND has retired tools that did not serve the Reciprocates (the pivot from Web3). The same posture applies to anything else that doesn't earn its place.

e. **Transparency from day one.** Governance documents, theory of change, strategic plan, and policies are all public before incorporation. This is the floor, not the ceiling.

f. **Honest about the unknowns.** Every document has an "open questions" section. Funders trust organizations that name their gaps before being asked.
