## How to read this document

The funder pipeline is HAND's master tracker for fundraising. Each entry includes:

- **Funder.** Name and program if relevant.
- **Fit.** Why this funder aligns with HAND.
- **Access path.** RFP, invitation-only, relationship-led, peer panel.
- **Range.** Typical grant size.
- **Relationship status.** Cold, warming, in conversation, applied, awarded, declined.
- **Next action.** What HAND does next, by when.
- **Notes.** Specific intelligence, due diligence, cautions.

The pipeline is **not** a public document. The repository carries the structural template; specific outreach status is maintained in the Executive Director's working copy.

## Section 1. Fit tiers

Funders are ranked by alignment with HAND's posture across three vectors:

a. **Trust-based posture.** Multi-year unrestricted, relational, minimal reporting burden.

b. **Mission alignment.** Capacity-building, accompaniment, grassroots, healers, impact entrepreneurs.

c. **AI sovereignty alignment.** Open-source AI, group-owned data, responsible AI infrastructure.

A funder strong on all three is a **Tier 1** prospect. Strong on two is **Tier 2**. Strong on one is **Tier 3**.

## Section 8. What HAND does not pursue

Honesty about what's outside the pipeline:

a. **Federal grants.** Compliance burden disproportionate to capacity at HAND's stage. Revisit at Year 4+.

b. **Corporate sponsorships** where mission alignment is weak. HAND prefers mission-aligned small business and B-Corp partnerships if they emerge organically.

c. **Crypto-specific grant programs** (Gitcoin, Optimism, etc.). HAND's pivot away from crypto-first positioning means these are not aligned. Crypto donations continue at the small inflow level via the donate-crypto page, but HAND does not pursue crypto-native funding rounds.

d. **AI vendor sponsorships** that compromise sovereignty principles. No "powered by [vendor]" relationships; vendor credits are operational tools, not partnership endorsements.

e. **Anything that would require modifying the eight Sovereign Reciprocates principles.** Funder pressure to do so is itself a signal of misalignment.

## Section 9. Relationship cultivation discipline

For each Tier 1 and Tier 2 funder:

a. **Touchpoint cadence** at least quarterly. Updates, invitations, useful content.

b. **No begging.** Reach out with substance (a published piece, an invitation, a question), not with an ask.

c. **Honor "no."** A declined application is feedback. Don't disappear; maintain the relationship.

d. **Track in a CRM.** Even a Google Sheet for now; graduate to HubSpot Nonprofit, Salesforce NPSP, or Bloomerang once volume warrants.

e. **The Executive Director (or founder pre-ED) owns Tier 1 relationships.** Don't delegate the depth-building to a development hire prematurely.
