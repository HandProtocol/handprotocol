# Officer Job Descriptions

> What each officer of HAND Protocol Foundation actually does. Adopted by Board resolution.

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · May 2026 · Pre-adoption

---

## Common to all officers

All officers of the Corporation:

a. Are elected annually by the Board at the annual meeting.

b. Serve a one-year term; eligible for re-election up to three consecutive one-year terms in the same office.

c. Receive no compensation for officer service (compensation for any employment role is governed by [`../policies/executive-compensation.md`](../policies/executive-compensation.md)).

d. May be removed by majority vote of the Board at any time.

e. Sign the annual Board Member Agreement and Conflict of Interest Disclosure.

f. Honor confidentiality and the Code of Ethics.

g. Act in accordance with the three fiduciary duties.

## Board Chair

**Texas BOC statutory title:** President (Texas requires "President"; HAND uses "Board Chair" in operational naming because the founder may at some point serve as a paid Executive Director who is colloquially "the president of HAND." Where Texas statute or external documents require "President," that term refers to this role.)

### Purpose

Leads the Board in governing the Corporation. Partners with the Executive Director to ensure the Corporation fulfills its mission.

### Core responsibilities

a. **Sets the Board agenda** in consultation with the Executive Director and Secretary.

b. **Presides at all Board meetings**, ensuring productive deliberation and timely decisions.

c. **Serves as the Board's principal liaison to the Executive Director**, communicating Board direction and feedback.

d. **Conducts the annual Executive Director performance review** with the Compensation Committee.

e. **Represents the Corporation publicly** in coordination with the Executive Director.

f. **Calls special meetings** as needed.

g. **Signs major contracts and documents** that require an officer signature (subject to the signing-authority policy).

h. **Mentors the Vice Chair** as the designated successor.

i. **Coordinates with committee chairs** on committee work and reporting.

j. **Leads the Board through transitions**, including succession planning.

### Reporting

Reports to the full Board at every meeting.

### Time commitment

Approximately **10–15 hours per month**, with more during transitions or strategic planning.

### Qualifications

a. Prior service on a nonprofit board strongly preferred.

b. Strong commitment to HAND's mission and values.

c. Experience leading meetings and shepherding group decisions.

d. Ability to give and receive direct feedback.

e. Available for between-meeting calls and decisions.

## Vice Chair

### Purpose

Acts in the absence or disability of the Board Chair. Serves as Chair-elect. Chairs the Governance Committee.

### Core responsibilities

a. **Performs the Chair's duties** when the Chair is unavailable.

b. **Chairs the Governance Committee**, leading board recruitment, orientation, evaluation, and Bylaws review.

c. **Apprentices for the Chair role**, attending key Chair conversations and gradually assuming responsibilities.

d. **Leads the annual board self-assessment**.

e. **Coordinates board orientation** for new directors.

f. **Reviews and updates** Bylaws and governance policies on a rolling basis.

g. **Co-leads** strategic planning with the Chair and ED.

### Reporting

Reports to the Board Chair and to the full Board.

### Time commitment

Approximately **8–12 hours per month**, more during board recruitment cycles or strategic planning.

### Qualifications

a. Commitment to mission.

b. Interest in eventually serving as Chair.

c. Strong listening and facilitation skills.

d. Patience for governance process work.

## Treasurer

### Purpose

Provides board-level oversight of the Corporation's financial affairs. Chairs the Finance Committee. Ensures financial accuracy, internal controls, and compliance.

### Core responsibilities

a. **Reviews monthly financial reports** prepared by the bookkeeper or staff.

b. **Chairs the Finance Committee**, scheduling meetings, setting agendas, ensuring deliverables.

c. **Reviews the proposed annual budget** with the Executive Director and brings it to the Board for approval.

d. **Presents quarterly financial reports** to the full Board.

e. **Signs the Form 990** annually (or delegates with Board approval).

f. **Oversees the external CPA relationship**, compilation, review, or audit per the Financial Management Policy.

g. **Reviews internal controls** and reports any material weaknesses to the Board.

h. **Co-signs checks and approves wire transfers** per the signing-authority policy.

i. **Oversees the rebuttable-presumption procedure** for Executive Director compensation.

j. **Reviews the Reserves and Investment posture** annually.

k. **Oversees insurance** with the Executive Director (carrier selection, renewal review).

l. **Ensures the Compliance Calendar** is current and being followed.

### Reporting

Reports to the full Board at every meeting on financial position, operations, and any internal-control matters.

### Time commitment

Approximately **8–12 hours per month**, more during budget season and audit time.

### Qualifications

a. **Financial fluency.** CPA or CFO background ideal; experienced nonprofit board treasurer acceptable. At minimum, comfort reading and explaining financial statements.

b. Comfort with nonprofit financial accounting (FASB ASC 958, restricted funds, in-kind valuations).

c. Strong attention to detail.

d. Willingness to ask hard questions about variances.

## Secretary

### Purpose

Maintains the Corporation's official records. Ensures proper notice of meetings. Certifies bylaws, resolutions, and other corporate actions.

### Core responsibilities

a. **Maintains corporate records**, including:
- Certificate of Formation and amendments.
- Bylaws and amendments.
- All Board and committee meeting minutes.
- Resolutions and certifications.
- Annual Disclosure Forms.
- Board Member Agreements.
- Other governance documents.

b. **Prepares meeting minutes** for every Board and committee meeting (may delegate to a staff member with Secretary review and approval).

c. **Ensures notice of meetings** is given per the Bylaws.

d. **Certifies bylaws, resolutions, and corporate actions** when requested by banks, funders, counsel, or other third parties.

e. **Maintains the directors' list and officer slate** with current contact information.

f. **Oversees compliance with the Document Retention Policy** as records custodian for corporate records.

g. **Files the Texas SOS Periodic Report** (Form 802) when requested.

h. **Maintains the Conflict of Interest disclosure file**.

### Reporting

Reports to the full Board on governance compliance and records matters.

### Time commitment

Approximately **5–8 hours per month**, more around meeting cycles.

### Qualifications

a. Organized and methodical.

b. Comfort with documentation discipline.

c. Discretion (handles confidential materials).

d. Familiarity with Texas nonprofit governance preferred but not required.

## Executive Director (when role is created)

The Executive Director is a chief executive employee, not a board officer. Included here because the role appears repeatedly in the officer descriptions above and its scope warrants articulation.

### Purpose

Chief executive of the Corporation. Reports to the Board. Leads staff and program operations. Implements the strategic plan adopted by the Board.

### Core responsibilities

a. **Implements** the strategic plan, programs, and Board direction.

b. **Manages staff and contractors**, including hiring, performance management, and Code of Ethics adherence.

c. **Leads fundraising** in partnership with the Board, including grant applications, donor stewardship, and campaigns.

d. **Manages Reciprocate program** delivery in partnership with program staff.

e. **Oversees Sovereign Reciprocates program** in coordination with the Oversight Committee.

f. **Represents** the Corporation publicly.

g. **Reports** to the Board at every meeting.

h. **Prepares** the annual budget for Board approval.

i. **Ensures** compliance with law, policies, and Bylaws.

j. **Develops** the Corporation's culture and team.

### Reporting

Reports to the Board, through the Board Chair as principal liaison.

### Time commitment

Full-time when role is funded and filled. Part-time or unpaid (founder) until then.

### Qualifications

a. **Mission alignment.** Deep commitment to HAND's mission and the Reciprocate-Contributor model.

b. **Operational excellence.** Track record managing nonprofit operations.

c. **Fundraising.** Successful experience in nonprofit fundraising at appropriate scale.

d. **Relational integrity.** Strong relationships with funders, peers, and grassroots organizations.

e. **Voice fit.** Plain English, evidence-based, honest about unknowns; aligned with the brand voice.

### Compensation

Set under [`../policies/executive-compensation.md`](../policies/executive-compensation.md) and the rebuttable-presumption procedure.

## Combinations and limits

a. The same person may not hold the offices of Board Chair (President) and Secretary, per Texas BOC §22.234.

b. The Executive Director is not an officer. The Executive Director attends Board meetings as a non-voting ex-officio participant and is excused for executive sessions on the Executive Director's evaluation, compensation, or employment.

c. The Treasurer and the Executive Director are typically different people, even at the smallest scale, to maintain the segregation of authority for financial matters.

d. Where the founder serves on the founding Board, the founder may serve as Board Chair for up to 24 months. See [`succession-and-replacement.md`](succession-and-replacement.md) §8.

## Open questions

- **Chief Strategy / Program Officer roles.** Not anticipated in the first 2-3 years. As staff scales, may add COO or CPO-equivalent. Officer status (or not) decided then.
- **Combined Treasurer/Secretary.** Permitted by Texas (only Chair/Secretary combination is barred). For HAND's first 3-director board, briefly combining may be necessary. Recommend splitting as soon as a fourth director is recruited.
- **Officer compensation in any form.** Drafted as fully volunteer. As HAND scales and officer responsibilities grow (especially Treasurer), modest stipends are an option in the 5-year horizon, with separate rebuttable-presumption documentation.
