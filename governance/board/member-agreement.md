# Board Member Agreement

> Signed annually by each director of HAND Protocol Foundation. Articulates the commitments a director makes by accepting a seat on the Board.

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · May 2026 · Pre-adoption

---

## Preamble

This Agreement summarizes the commitments and expectations of service on the Board of Directors of HAND Protocol Foundation (the "**Corporation**"). It is signed by each director annually at or before the first regular Board meeting of the fiscal year. Signing this Agreement is a precondition for continued voting service.

This Agreement does not create an employment relationship. Directors serve in a fiduciary capacity, voluntarily and without compensation, except as expressly provided in the Bylaws.

## 1. Fiduciary commitments

I commit to act in accordance with the **three fiduciary duties** of a director under Texas law:

a. **Duty of care.** I will be informed, attend meetings prepared, exercise the care of an ordinarily prudent person in like circumstances, and apply independent judgment.

b. **Duty of loyalty.** I will place the interests of the Corporation above my own interests, business relationships, and personal preferences. I will disclose and recuse on conflicts of interest.

c. **Duty of obedience.** I will act consistent with the Corporation's mission, the Bylaws, the Certificate of Formation, and applicable law.

## 2. Time and attendance

I commit to:

a. Attend at least **75%** of regular Board meetings during my term, in person or remotely.

b. Read materials provided in advance of each meeting.

c. Serve on at least **one standing committee** and attend that committee's meetings reliably.

d. Make myself reasonably available for between-meeting communications appropriate to the Corporation's stage.

e. Allocate roughly **6 to 10 hours per month** in HAND's first year (longer at moments of transition, less in steady state).

## 3. Mission and values

I commit to:

a. Understand HAND's mission, theory of change, and strategic plan, and refresh my understanding as those documents evolve.

b. Use the term **Reciprocates** for the population HAND serves, and **Contributors** for the skill-givers, in all formal communications about the Corporation.

c. Honor HAND's brand voice: earnest, evidence-based, relational. Plain English over institutional language.

d. Honor the Sovereign Reciprocates sovereignty commitments.

## 4. Governance documents

I acknowledge that I have received, read, and agree to abide by:

a. The Bylaws.

b. The Conflict of Interest Policy.

c. The Whistleblower Policy.

d. The Document Retention and Destruction Policy.

e. The Code of Ethics.

f. The Equal Opportunity and Anti-Harassment Policy.

g. The Privacy Policy.

h. The Data Sovereignty and AI Policy.

I commit to comply with any additional policies adopted by the Board during my term.

## 5. Conflict of interest disclosure

I have completed the annual Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form ([`annual-disclosure-form.md`](annual-disclosure-form.md)). I will update my disclosure promptly if any material change occurs during the year. I will disclose actual or potential conflicts at any time they arise in connection with a Board or committee matter, and will recuse from deliberation and vote where appropriate.

## 6. Confidentiality

I will hold in confidence all Board deliberations, personnel matters, Reciprocate or Contributor information, and other matters that are not part of the public record of the Corporation. The duty of confidentiality survives my service as a director.

## 7. Public representation

I will:

a. Identify myself as a director of HAND Protocol Foundation only when authorized and appropriate.

b. Frame personal opinions as personal, not as the position of the Corporation.

c. Refer media inquiries to the Executive Director or Board Chair unless I have been authorized to speak on a specific matter.

d. Refrain from public criticism of fellow directors, staff, Reciprocates, Contributors, donors, or peer organizations. Disagreements I address through Board channels.

## 8. Fundraising

I commit to:

a. Support HAND's fundraising as one of the "give / get / get out of the way" expectations for directors: I will make an annual personally meaningful gift, help open doors to other prospects, or both.

b. Participate in the annual fundraising plan as approved by the Board.

c. Honor donor stewardship commitments.

The Board expects each director to make an annual personally meaningful gift (the amount is the director's own determination; meaningfulness is the criterion, not a dollar threshold). 100% board giving is a measurable signal to funders.

## 9. Financial oversight

I commit to:

a. Review monthly or quarterly financial reports.

b. Understand the annual budget I vote to approve.

c. Ask questions when financial reports are unclear.

d. Support adoption and enforcement of financial controls in [`../policies/financial-management.md`](../policies/financial-management.md).

## 10. Strategic engagement

I commit to:

a. Participate in annual strategic planning.

b. Bring forward strategic observations or concerns to the Board.

c. Resist the urge to manage rather than govern: leave operational decisions to staff and focus my time on strategy, oversight, and stewardship.

## 11. Indemnification and protection

The Corporation will indemnify me to the fullest extent permitted by Texas law and will maintain Directors and Officers (D&O) liability insurance during my service. Limitations and procedures are in Bylaws Article VIII.

## 12. Term and removal

a. My term is **three (3) years** unless I am filling a partial term.

b. I may serve **two consecutive three-year terms**, with a minimum one-year break before re-election.

c. I may resign at any time by written notice.

d. I may be removed for cause or without cause by two-thirds (2/3) vote of the other directors, per Bylaws §4.6.

e. I commit to a graceful departure if removed or term-limited: complete pending committee work, transition relationships and files, and refrain from public criticism of the decision.

## 13. Founder-aware commitments

If I serve alongside the founder of HAND:

a. I will not permit the founder's voice to substitute for the Board's collective judgment, regardless of the founder's role on the Board.

b. I will participate in honest assessment of founder performance, including any future Executive Director role the founder takes on.

c. I will participate in founder transition planning per [`succession-and-replacement.md`](succession-and-replacement.md).

## 14. Acknowledgment

By signing below, I acknowledge that:

a. I have received and read this Agreement and the documents referenced.

b. I understand the commitments I am making.

c. I commit to fulfilling them to the best of my ability.

d. I will notify the Board Chair promptly if my ability to fulfill these commitments materially changes.

## Open questions

- **Give-or-get target.** Drafted as "personally meaningful" without a dollar floor. Some peer orgs use $1,000 minimum or a "give-or-get" target like $5,000. Decide whether to add a minimum at adoption.
- **Founder-specific provisions.** Drafted to apply if I serve alongside the founder. Could be made stronger as the org matures and the founder transitions out of board service.
- **Term-limit waivers.** Drafted with strict 2x3-year limits. Some orgs allow board-supermajority waiver in exceptional cases. Consider for the next revision.
