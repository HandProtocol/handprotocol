---

## Cover instructions

This form is completed at or before the first regular Board meeting of each fiscal year. You may also update your disclosure at any time during the year if material circumstances change.

Signed forms are retained by the Secretary per the Document Retention Policy. Forms are confidential and accessible only to the Secretary, the Board Chair, the Audit Committee Chair, and, on a need-to-know basis, outside counsel.
