# Emergency Succession Plan

> The detailed operating plan if the Executive Director suddenly becomes unable to fulfill the role. Companion document to [`succession-and-replacement.md`](succession-and-replacement.md) §2.

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · May 2026 · Pre-adoption · Review annually

---

## How this document works

This is an **operating plan**, not a policy. The policy is in [`succession-and-replacement.md`](succession-and-replacement.md). This document fills in the names, contacts, account details, and step-by-step actions needed in the first 48 hours and first 90 days of an emergency.

**Confidentiality:** Some content (account numbers, passwords, contact details) is sensitive. The full version of this plan is stored in a Board-only secure location. The version in the repository carries the structure and the placeholder fields.

**Annual review:** The Treasurer and Board Chair review this plan annually and update any changed fields. The review date is logged at the top of this document.

---

## Section 1. Notification chain

If the Executive Director is incapacitated, deceased, or has abruptly departed:

| Order | Who notifies whom | Within | By what means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whoever discovers the situation → Board Chair | Immediately | Phone first, email backup |
| 2 | Board Chair → Vice Chair | 1 hour | Phone |
| 3 | Board Chair → Treasurer | 1 hour | Phone |
| 4 | Board Chair → Secretary | 1 hour | Phone |
| 5 | Board Chair → other directors | 6 hours | Phone or video meeting |
| 6 | Executive Committee → staff and contractors | 24 hours | In-person, video, or phone (not email-only for first notification) |
| 7 | Acting ED + Board Chair → key funders and donors | 48 hours | Personal call or video, follow-up email |
| 8 | Acting ED + Board Chair → active Companion groups | 72 hours | Personal call or video |
| 9 | Acting ED + Board Chair → public statement if warranted | 72 hours | Coordinated drafting |

### Acting ED authority

Per [`succession-and-replacement.md`](succession-and-replacement.md) §2, the Acting ED has full operational authority for up to 90 days, with these exceptions requiring Board approval:

- New hires
- Terminations beyond routine
- Contracts above $25,000
- Material program changes
- Public statements on behalf of the Corporation

## Section 7. First 48 hours operating checklist

| ☐ | Action |
|---|---|
| ☐ | Confirm the situation; verify Acting ED designation; activate Acting ED |
| ☐ | Convene Executive Committee emergency meeting |
| ☐ | Notify Board members |
| ☐ | Lock down social-media and email accounts; confirm authorized speakers |
| ☐ | Confirm payroll-current status; defer any non-essential payments for 7 days |
| ☐ | Confirm grant-reporting deadlines for next 60 days; flag any at risk |
| ☐ | Notify staff and contractors in person where possible |
| ☐ | Notify key funders and major donors with proactive call |
| ☐ | Notify active Companion groups personally |
| ☐ | Draft public statement if needed; have Board Chair review before publishing |
| ☐ | Document everything: timeline, decisions, communications |
| ☐ | Apply legal hold to all records (Document Retention §7) |
| ☐ | Notify insurance broker (D&O policy may require notice) |
| ☐ | Consult counsel if any legal or employment complications |

## Section 8. First 30 days operating checklist

| ☐ | Action |
|---|---|
| ☐ | Board Response Committee appointed and meeting weekly |
| ☐ | Stabilize routine operations under Acting ED |
| ☐ | Continue reporting deadlines and donor stewardship without interruption |
| ☐ | Initiate search process (interim ED or permanent ED) |
| ☐ | Conduct staff and Contributor check-ins; address concerns |
| ☐ | Maintain Companion-group communications |
| ☐ | Review and adjust budget for the transition period |
| ☐ | Update website and public materials as appropriate |
| ☐ | First 30-day status report to full Board |

## Section 9. First 90 days operating checklist

| ☐ | Action |
|---|---|
| ☐ | Permanent or interim ED selection complete or near-complete |
| ☐ | Acting ED transition plan to successor in place |
| ☐ | Full Board strategic review of the transition |
| ☐ | Funder relationships restabilized |
| ☐ | Companion-group continuity verified |
| ☐ | Lessons learned documented |
| ☐ | This Plan updated based on real-world experience |

## Section 10. Plan maintenance

a. **Annual review** by Treasurer and Board Chair. Date logged at top of document.

b. **Cross-train sessions** quarterly: each function in the Backup Matrix exercises the backup once per quarter.

c. **Update on change.** Any change in roles, contracts, account access, or relationships triggers an update within 30 days.

d. **Tabletop exercise.** Annually, the Executive Committee runs a 60-minute tabletop exercise simulating an emergency, to test the Plan and surface gaps.

## Section 11. Storage and access

a. The full Plan (with names, contacts, account references) is stored:
- In the Board's secured shared drive (access: directors and officers).
- In a paper copy held by the Board Chair.
- Referenced from the password manager (Section 6).

b. The redacted version (this document, without sensitive details) is in the repository for public visibility into governance.

c. **Disaster scenario:** if all electronic systems are inaccessible, the Board Chair's paper copy is the authoritative version.
