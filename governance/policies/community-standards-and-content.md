# Community Standards and Content Moderation Policy

> Covers what HAND Protocol Foundation does and does not allow in its community spaces (Discord, mailing list, public comment surfaces), what content guardrails apply to the Sovereign Companions AI output, and how user-submitted content is accepted, declined, or removed.

**Status:** Draft v0.1 · May 2026 · Pre-adoption

---

## Section 1 — Purpose

HAND Protocol Foundation operates several spaces where people communicate: the Discord server, the mailing list, the public website (with comments or feedback surfaces when present), and any future Companion / Contributor portals. The Sovereign Companions program also produces AI-generated content that may be shared externally. This Policy:

a. Sets expectations for behavior in HAND community spaces.

b. Defines content that is not allowed.

c. Establishes guardrails on AI output produced by Sovereign Companions agents.

d. Establishes the acceptance, decline, and removal process for user-submitted content.

e. Addresses appeals, copyright claims, and abuse reports.

## Section 2 — Scope

This Policy applies to:

a. **Discord server** (`discord.handprotocol.org`).

b. **Mailing list** and any direct reply channels.

c. **Public website** (handprotocol.org) and any sub-surfaces hosting user comments, public forums, or submissions.

d. **Sovereign Companions agent output** shared externally on behalf of Companion groups.

e. **Social media accounts** operated by HAND (X / Twitter, future surfaces).

f. **Conduct at HAND-hosted events** (online or in person), to the extent the conduct occurs in HAND spaces or affects HAND community members.

This Policy does **not** govern:

- Personal social-media accounts of individuals affiliated with HAND (governed by Code of Ethics §9).
- Content on Companion groups' own platforms produced with their own resources (governed by the Companion group's own policies; HAND has no authority over them).

## Section 3 — Community standards

In HAND community spaces, participants commit to:

a. **Respect.** Treat every other participant with dignity, even in disagreement.

b. **Honesty.** Communicate honestly. Do not impersonate, misrepresent credentials, or deceive.

c. **Relevance.** Stay roughly on-topic for the channel or thread.

d. **Patience.** Recognize that HAND community members vary in technical literacy, English fluency, and prior exposure to the topics under discussion.

e. **Privacy.** Do not share another person's identifying or private information without consent.

f. **Constructive disagreement.** Disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Section 4 — Prohibited content

The following content is prohibited in HAND community spaces and will be removed:

a. **Harassment and targeted abuse.** Personal attacks, threats, intimidation, or sustained antagonism toward identifiable individuals.

b. **Discrimination and dehumanization.** Content that demeans people based on protected characteristics under the Equal Opportunity and Anti-Harassment Policy.

c. **Doxxing.** Sharing another person's home address, phone number, immigration status, employer, family member identities, or other private information without consent.

d. **Threats and incitement.** Specific threats of violence or content that incites violence against identifiable people or groups.

e. **Sexual content involving minors.** Any such content is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and to law enforcement per applicable law.

f. **Sexually explicit content** in HAND community spaces, even between consenting adults, because the spaces include audiences for whom such content is unwelcome and inappropriate.

g. **Spam, scams, phishing, and malware.** Including unsolicited commercial promotion, recruiting for unrelated organizations, scam links, and malicious files.

h. **Impersonation.** Pretending to be HAND staff, another community member, or a public figure.

i. **Commercial solicitation** beyond what HAND has explicitly authorized for the channel (e.g., a Contributor advertising their unrelated business in a Companion-support channel).

j. **Crisis exploitation.** Using a community member's expressed distress or vulnerability for commercial gain, attention, or political leverage.

k. **Misinformation that materially harms.** Claims demonstrably false about medical care, legal rights, financial decisions, or public safety where the false claim could lead someone to harmful action.

l. **Content that violates law.** Including copyright infringement, defamation, threats, or illegal goods/services.

## Section 5 — Enforcement

a. **Removal.** Content violating Section 4 is removed by moderators. The person who posted is notified with a brief explanation.

b. **Warning.** A first violation typically results in removal plus a warning, unless the content is severe (e.g., threats, doxxing).

c. **Temporary suspension.** Repeated or moderate violations result in temporary suspension from the affected space (typically 24 hours to 30 days).

d. **Permanent removal.** Severe violations, or repeated violations after warning, result in permanent removal.

e. **Appeals.** A removed member may appeal to the Executive Director by email. The Executive Director reviews and responds within 7 days. Further appeal to the Board Chair if the matter is material.

f. **Documentation.** Moderation actions are logged, including the content removed (or hash/reference if removal was for severity), the moderator, the rationale, and the disposition.

## Section 6 — Moderators

a. **Designation.** Moderators are HAND staff or specifically designated Contributors with moderation training.

b. **Authority.** Moderators may remove content and apply temporary suspensions consistent with this Policy. Permanent removals require Executive Director sign-off.

c. **Conflict of interest.** A moderator with a personal stake in a content dispute (e.g., the content concerns the moderator) recuses; another moderator handles.

d. **Transparency.** The list of current moderators is published in the community space.

## Section 7 — Sovereign Companions content guardrails

Sovereign Companions agents produce text and other content on behalf of Companion groups. These guardrails apply before any external publication:

a. **Human-review gate.** No external output ships without explicit human review by either the Companion group or, in initial stages, HAND staff with Companion-group consent. This is a sovereignty principle (principle 6 audit trail; implicit in the program's design) operationalized at the publishing layer.

b. **No clinical, legal, or financial advice in agent voice.** Agent output may help draft documents, summarize, brainstorm, or research. Output framed as professional advice (medical diagnosis, legal opinion, financial recommendation) is prohibited. Where output relates to such domains, it is clearly framed as draft material for the Companion group to review with appropriate professionals.

c. **Source citation.** Output incorporating retrieved sources cites them so the Companion group can verify.

d. **Hallucination check.** Output containing specific factual claims (dates, numbers, names, statistics, regulations) is flagged for source verification before publication.

e. **Content that is prohibited under Section 4.** Same prohibitions apply to agent output. Output that would violate Section 4 is filtered and logged for review.

f. **Companion-group voice preservation.** Output preserves and respects the Companion group's voice. The agent serves the group, not the other way around.

g. **Transparency about AI involvement.** Where agent output is shared with parties outside the Companion group (funders, peer organizations, the public), the AI involvement is disclosed in a manner appropriate to context.

h. **No external use without consent.** Agent output about a Companion group is not used for HAND's marketing, fundraising, or peer-org communication without explicit Companion-group consent.

i. **Specific prohibited use cases:**
- Generating content that targets identifiable individuals for harassment or pressure.
- Generating content that misrepresents the Companion group's positions to external audiences.
- Generating content for high-stakes external decisions (grant submissions, legal filings, public statements during crisis) without explicit human authorship and review.

## Section 8 — Algorithmic Impact Assessment

Before deploying any Sovereign Companions agent in a new context, the Sovereign Companions Oversight Committee conducts an Algorithmic Impact Assessment, including:

a. Intended use and intended users.

b. Who could be harmed by failures, and how.

c. What guardrails apply.

d. What review processes apply.

e. How errors are surfaced and corrected.

f. How the Companion group can override or revoke.

## Section 9 — User-submitted content

User-submitted content (testimonials, case studies, Contributor portfolios, public submissions of any kind) is reviewed before publication on HAND surfaces.

a. **Acceptance criteria.**
- Consistent with HAND's mission and values.
- Submitted by a verified person with rights to share the content.
- Not prohibited under Section 4.
- Sufficiently anonymized if it includes information about third parties.

b. **Decline criteria.**
- Prohibited content (Section 4).
- Materially misleading.
- Insufficient consent for any third parties named.
- Outside scope of HAND's public surfaces.

c. **Decline communication.** The submitter is notified of the decline and the reason within 14 days. Appeals to the Executive Director.

d. **Permission and license.** Submitters retain copyright. By submitting, they grant HAND a non-exclusive license to publish on HAND surfaces in connection with HAND's mission, until withdrawn. Withdrawals honored within 14 days.

## Section 10 — Copyright and DMCA

a. **Designated Agent.** The Corporation has designated a DMCA agent registered with the U.S. Copyright Office. Contact information published on the website at `/dmca`.

b. **Takedown notices.** Compliant DMCA takedown notices (17 U.S.C. §512) are processed promptly: content removed pending counter-notice; submitter notified.

c. **Counter-notices.** Counter-notices addressed per the statute; content restored 10-14 business days after counter-notice unless the original claimant pursues court action.

d. **Repeat infringer policy.** Members with multiple validated takedowns are removed from community spaces.

## Section 11 — Abuse and emergency reporting

a. **Reporting.** Any community member may report abuse to **abuse@handprotocol.org** or to a moderator.

b. **Anonymous reports.** Accepted.

c. **Acknowledgment.** Within 24 hours for severe reports, 5 business days for routine.

d. **Emergencies.** Threats of imminent harm reported to local emergency services. The Corporation cooperates with law enforcement on validly served process.

e. **Safety planning.** If a community member appears to be in a crisis (self-harm, suicide ideation, intimate-partner violence, etc.), moderators escalate to the Executive Director and follow a documented response protocol that prioritizes the person's safety and connects to professional resources. Moderators do not provide professional crisis counseling themselves.

## Section 12 — Off-platform conduct

a. Conduct outside HAND community spaces is generally outside HAND's authority.

b. Exception: where a person's off-platform conduct involves another HAND community member and creates safety risk inside HAND spaces, HAND may take protective action consistent with this Policy.

c. The Corporation does not investigate or adjudicate off-platform disputes.

## Section 13 — Specific platform notes

### Discord

a. Moderation tools used per Discord's terms of service.

b. Channel structure documented in the server's pinned welcome message.

c. Roles assigned based on relationship (Companion, Contributor, donor, public).

d. Server logs retained per Document Retention Policy.

### Mailing list

a. Replies to mailing-list emails routed to a moderated inbox.

b. Unsubscribe links honored within 5 business days.

c. Resend Audiences and the `subscribe.js` function are the back-end; data handling per Privacy Policy.

### Website surfaces

a. Public-facing comments not currently enabled on the website.

b. Should public comments be enabled, comment moderation falls under this Policy.

## Section 14 — Annual review

The Executive Director (or designee) reviews this Policy annually, considering:

a. Volume and patterns of moderation actions.

b. Appeals and outcomes.

c. New platform or content categories.

d. Changes in law or platform terms.

e. Recommendations from moderators and from the Sovereign Companions Oversight Committee.

## Open questions

- **AI content disclosure norms.** The field is evolving. Default position: disclose AI involvement when the output represents an organization's voice externally. Adjust as norms harden.
- **Moderator compensation.** Volunteer moderators or paid? Pre-revenue, almost certainly volunteer. Reassess at staff scale.
- **Public-comment surfaces.** Not currently enabled. If enabled (e.g., a public discussion thread on the discovery docs), pre-define moderation cadence before launch.
- **Discord vs. Matrix vs. Slack.** Discord chosen for current reach. Some Companion groups may prefer Signal-based communication for safety reasons. Tactical, not policy.
- **Hash-based archives.** For removed severe content, hash-based archives may be necessary for accountability and pattern detection. Logistics deferred.
